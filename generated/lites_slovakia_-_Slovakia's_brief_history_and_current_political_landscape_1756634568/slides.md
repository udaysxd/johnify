## A Glimpse into Slovakia's Past
- Ancient roots in Great Moravia
- Part of the Austro-Hungarian Empire
- Brief existence as Czechoslovakia
- Post-WWII under Soviet influence
- Velvet Divorce: Peaceful split from Czech Republic

<!-- NARRATION: Slovakia boasts a rich history, stretching back to the influential Great Moravian Empire. It later became part of the vast Austro-Hungarian Empire, experiencing centuries of shared history before a brief period within Czechoslovakia. -->

## The Road to Independence
- Czechoslovakia formed after WWI
- Post-WWII, communist rule intensified
- The Prague Spring of 1968 crushed
- Velvet Revolution in 1989 led to democracy
- Peaceful dissolution of Czechoslovakia in 1993

<!-- NARRATION: The journey to modern Slovakia was marked by significant events, including the formation and eventual dissolution of Czechoslovakia. The peaceful Velvet Revolution in 1989 paved the way for democratic reforms. -->

---

## Modern Slovakia Emerges
- Independent Slovak Republic established 1993
- Joined NATO in 2004
- Joined the European Union in 2004
- Adopted the Euro in 2009
- Transition to a market economy

<!-- NARRATION: Following a peaceful 'Velvet Divorce', the independent Slovak Republic was born in 1993. Slovakia has since embraced its European future, joining both NATO and the European Union, and adopting the Euro. -->

## Contemporary Political Structure
- Unitary parliamentary republic
- President as head of state
- Prime Minister leads the government
- Unicameral National Council of the Slovak Republic
- Multi-party system

<!-- NARRATION: Today, Slovakia operates as a unitary parliamentary republic, with a President as head of state and a Prime Minister leading the government. Its unicameral legislature, the National Council, represents a multi-party democratic system. -->
