# Key Concepts for you

Here are the concepts you might want to review:

---

## What is the capital of Germany?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** Berlin


**Explanation:** Berlin is the capital and largest city of Germany\. Munich, Hamburg, and Frankfurt are major cities in Germany but not the capital\.

---

## Which famous landmark is located in Berlin?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** Brandenburg Gate


**Explanation:** The Brandenburg Gate is an 18th\-century neoclassical monument in Berlin\. The Eiffel Tower is in Paris, the Colosseum in Rome, and Big Ben in London\.

---

## What river flows through Berlin?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** Spree


**Explanation:** The Spree River flows through Berlin\. The Danube, Rhine, and Elbe are major rivers in Germany but do not flow through Berlin\.
