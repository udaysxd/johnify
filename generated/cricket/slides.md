# Key Concepts for you

Here are the concepts you might want to review:

---

## What is the maximum number of players allowed in a cricket team on the field at any one time?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** 11


**Explanation:** A cricket team consists of 11 players on the field at any one time\. This is a standard rule in cricket, ensuring the game is played with a balanced number of participants\.

---

## Which of the following is not a format of international cricket?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** Triple Century


**Explanation:** Test, One Day International \(ODI\), and Twenty20 \(T20\) are all recognized formats of international cricket\. 'Triple Century' refers to a batsman scoring 300 or more runs in a single innings, not a format of the game\.

---

## How many balls are there in an over in cricket?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** 6


**Explanation:** An over in cricket consists of 6 balls bowled by the same bowler from one end of the pitch\. This is a fundamental rule that applies across all formats of the game\.
