## Welcome to the Dance Floor!
- Let's learn a fun, simple dance sequence.
- Perfect for beginners, no experience needed!
- Get ready to move and groove.
- We'll break it down step-by-step.

<!-- NARRATION: Hello dancers! Today we're going to learn a super easy and fun 3-step dance sequence that anyone can do. Get ready to shake off any nerves and embrace the joy of movement! -->

---

## Step 1: The Step-Touch
- Start with your feet together.
- Step to the right with your right foot.
- Touch your left foot to the right foot.
- Now, step to the left with your left foot.
- Touch your right foot to the left foot.

<!-- NARRATION: Our first step is the 'Step-Touch'. We'll start with our feet together, then step to the right with our right foot and bring our left foot to meet it. Then we'll reverse it, stepping left and touching with the right. -->

---

## Step 2: The Clap
- After your step-touch sequence, get ready to clap.
- Clap your hands together on the beat.
- This adds a rhythmic flourish.
- Let's practice: Step-touch, step-touch, clap!

<!-- NARRATION: Next up is the 'Clap'! After completing the step-touch on each side, we'll bring our hands together for a crisp clap. This adds a wonderful rhythmic element to our dance. -->

---

## Step 3: The Turn
- After the clap, it's time for a turn.
- Pivot on the balls of your feet.
- Make a quarter turn to the right.
- Continue the sequence from the new direction.

<!-- NARRATION: For our final step, we'll add a simple 'Turn'. After the clap, pivot smoothly on the balls of your feet to make a quarter turn to your right, ready to start the sequence again. -->

---

## Putting It All Together!
- Sequence: Step-touch right, touch left, step-touch left, touch right, clap, turn right.
- Repeat the whole sequence.
- Feel the rhythm and have fun!
- You've mastered a simple dance!

<!-- NARRATION: Now let's put it all together: step-touch, step-touch, clap, and turn! Keep repeating this to music and feel the flow. Congratulations, you've just learned a fantastic dance sequence! -->
