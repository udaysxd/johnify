## Introduction to lata mangeshkar career
- **Lata Mangeshkar** was a legendary Indian playback singer, often called the **"Nightingale of India,"** with a career spanning over **seven decades** in Bollywood and regional cinema.
- Her career is **culturally significant** as she recorded over **25,000 songs** in 36 languages, shaping the sound of Indian cinema and inspiring generations of artists.
- An **astonishing fact**: Lata Mangeshkar holds the **Guinness World Record** for the most recorded artist in history, with estimates of up to **50,000 solo, duet, and chorus-backed songs**.
