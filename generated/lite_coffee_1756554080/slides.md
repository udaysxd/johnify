## Coffee
- **Brewing ratio**: Use 60 grams of coffee per 1 liter of water (a 1:16.67 ratio) for a balanced cup.
- **Grind size matters**: For a French press, use a coarse grind; for espresso, use a fine, powdery grind.
- **Optimal water temperature**: Brew between 195°F and 205°F (90°C–96°C) to extract flavors properly without bitterness.
- **Storage tip**: Keep beans in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture; use within 2 weeks of roasting.
- **Global production**: Brazil is the world's largest producer, growing over 2.6 million metric tons of coffee annually.

## Core Ideas
- **Brewing temperature matters**: Optimal extraction occurs between 195–205°F (90–96°C) for most methods.
- **Grind size dictates method**: Use fine grind for espresso, medium for drip, and coarse for French press.
- **Coffee-to-water ratio is key**: A standard starting point is 1:15 (e.g., 20g coffee to 300g water).
- **Freshness has a timeline**: Ground coffee loses optimal flavor in ~30 minutes; whole beans last ~2 weeks.
- **Water quality impacts taste**: Use filtered water with ~150 ppm mineral content for balanced extraction.

## How To Learn
- **Start with a 1:15 coffee-to-water ratio** (e.g., 20g coffee for 300ml water) for balanced extraction.
- **Grind beans fresh** to a consistency like table salt for drip brewing; finer for espresso, coarser for French press.
- **Use water just off the boil** (around 93-96°C) to avoid under or over-extracting the coffee grounds.
- **Time your brew**: aim for 4 minutes for a French press or 2-3 minutes for a pour-over to control strength.
- **Taste and adjust** one variable at a time (e.g., grind size or ratio) based on bitterness or sourness.

## Examples
- **Brewing ratio**: Use 60g coffee per 1L water (1:16.67 ratio) for balanced extraction.
- **Grind size**: Espresso requires fine grind (like table salt); French press needs coarse (like breadcrumbs).
- **Water temperature**: Optimal range is 195–205°F (90–96°C) to avoid under or over-extraction.
- **Bloom time**: Pour just enough water to saturate grounds and let sit for 30 seconds to release CO₂.
- **Steeping time**: For a French press, steep for 4 minutes before plunging slowly.

## Summary
- **Brewing ratio**: Use 60g coffee per 1L water (1:16.67 ratio) for balanced extraction.
- **Grind size matters**: Espresso requires fine grind (30 sec shot), French press needs coarse (4 min steep).
- **Water temperature**: Ideal range is 195–205°F (90–96°C) to avoid under or over-extraction.
- **Storage tip**: Store beans in airtight container, away from light; use within 2 weeks of roasting.
- **Tasting notes**: Identify acidity (brightness), body (mouthfeel), and flavor (e.g., chocolate, fruit).
