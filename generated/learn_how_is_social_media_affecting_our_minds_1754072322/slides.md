## Introduction to how is social media affecting our minds?
- **How is social media affecting our minds?** Social media **reshapes attention spans, emotions, and social behaviors** by constantly exposing us to curated content, instant feedback, and algorithmic-driven engagement.
- **Why does it matter?** Understanding these effects helps us **navigate digital habits mindfully** and mitigate risks like anxiety, comparison, and misinformation.
- **Did you know?** Studies show the **average person spends 2.5 hours daily on social media**, with teens checking platforms **up to 100 times a day**—altering brain reward systems similarly to addictive behaviors.

- **Attention Fragmentation**: Social media encourages **constant switching** between tasks, reducing our ability to focus deeply. This matters because **sustained attention** is crucial for learning and productivity.
- **Dopamine-Driven Feedback Loops**: Likes and notifications trigger **dopamine releases**, making us crave more engagement. This matters because it can lead to **addictive behaviors** and reduced satisfaction with offline activities.

## Examples of how is social media affecting our minds?
- **Fear of Missing Out (FOMO)** – Social media exposes users to curated highlights of others' lives, creating anxiety about missing experiences. This **heightens stress** and can lead to compulsive checking of platforms.
- **Shortened Attention Spans** – Platforms like TikTok and Instagram prioritize **quick, engaging content**, training brains to seek constant stimulation. This makes it harder to focus on longer tasks like reading or deep work.
- **Comparison and Self-Esteem Issues** – Seeing idealized images of beauty or success can lead to **negative self-comparisons**. Studies link heavy social media use to increased rates of depression and body dissatisfaction.
