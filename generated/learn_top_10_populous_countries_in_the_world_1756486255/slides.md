## Top 10 Populous Countries In The World
*   **1. India:** Surpasses China as the world's most populous nation with over 1.428 billion people, representing nearly 18% of the global total.
*   **2. China:** Now second with just over 1.425 billion people; its population began declining in 2022 after decades of growth.
*   **3. United States:** A distant third with approximately 340 million people, driven by both natural increase and international migration.
*   **4. Indonesia:** The most populous country in Southeast Asia, with a population of over 277 million spread across its vast archipelago.
*   **5. Pakistan:** Population exceeds 240 million, with a high growth rate that is projected to see it rise in the rankings.
*   **6–10. Nigeria, Brazil, Bangladesh, Russia, Mexico:** These nations round out the list, with populations ranging from 223 million (Nigeria) to 128 million (Mexico).
