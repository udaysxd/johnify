## Introduction to why casteism sucks in india
- Casteism is a **discriminatory social hierarchy** that divides people by birth, denying equal opportunities and human dignity to millions of Indians based solely on their caste identity
- It remains critically relevant because it **perpetuates systemic inequality** in education, employment, and social mobility, undermining India's constitutional promise of equality and justice for all citizens
- Despite being outlawed for decades, government data shows **violence against Dalits** occurs every 15 minutes, with over 50,000 registered caste-based crimes in just the last five years alone

## Key Concepts
- **Systemic discrimination** creates unequal access to education, jobs, and resources based solely on birth, which perpetuates poverty and limits social mobility for millions of people.
- **Social segregation** enforces harmful divisions and restricts personal freedoms, preventing meaningful relationships and fostering prejudice that weakens community bonds and national unity.
- **Violence and oppression** are used to maintain caste hierarchy, leading to brutal attacks, honor killings, and fear that denies basic dignity and safety to marginalized groups.

## How why casteism sucks in india Works
- **Discrimination in daily life** restricts access to public spaces and services for lower castes, reinforcing social exclusion and denying basic human dignity through practices like untouchability.
- **Systemic educational and economic barriers** limit opportunities for lower castes through biased hiring and admission practices, perpetuating cycles of poverty and limiting national progress.
- **Violence and social control** are used to enforce caste hierarchy through honor killings and threats, creating a climate of fear that prevents social mobility and violates fundamental rights.

## Examples of why casteism sucks in india
- **Manual scavenging deaths** - Dalits are forced to clean human waste without protective gear, leading to frequent fatalities from toxic gases. This demonstrates how casteism **traps communities in deadly occupations** based on birth rather than choice or safety.
- **Inter-caste marriage violence** - Couples from different castes face honor killings, social boycotts, and violent attacks from their own families and communities. This shows how casteism **destroys personal freedom** and denies basic human rights to love and choose partners.
- **Caste-based educational discrimination** - Reserved seats in education often face backlash, and lower-caste students frequently experience social exclusion, bullying, and even suicide in premier institutions. This highlights how casteism **perpetuates educational inequality** and creates hostile learning environments.

## Summary: why casteism sucks in india
- Casteism **perpetuates systemic inequality** by restricting opportunities based on birth, not merit. This creates a deeply unfair society where millions are denied access to education, jobs, and social mobility simply because of their caste identity, wasting immense human potential and holding back national progress.
- It **normalizes discrimination and violence**, treating certain groups as "untouchable" or inferior. This leads to horrific human rights abuses, social boycotts, and hate crimes, fundamentally violating the constitutional promise of equality and dignity for all citizens.
- The practice **undermines national unity** by dividing society into rigid, hierarchical groups. It fosters prejudice and prevents the development of a collective Indian identity based on shared citizenship, weakening social cohesion and making it harder to solve common challenges.
