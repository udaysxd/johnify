## How To Learn
- **Spaced Repetition**: Review material at increasing intervals (e.g., 1 day, 3 days, 1 week) to improve long-term retention by over 200%.
- **Active Recall**: Test yourself without notes; studies show this boosts memory by up to 50% compared to passive rereading.
- **Interleaving**: Mix different subjects or types of problems in one session to enhance problem-solving skills by 25%.
- **Elaboration**: Explain concepts in your own words and connect them to prior knowledge, deepening understanding.
- **Dual Coding**: Combine words with visuals like diagrams or mind maps; this can double recall ability compared to text alone.
