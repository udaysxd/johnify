![What phenomenon is primarily responsible for the blue color of the sky](https://picsum.photos/seed/6fbf0cb3794097c042dc52695a8b5e84/800/300)

**Question:** What phenomenon is primarily responsible for the blue color of the sky?

**Correct Answer:** Rayleigh scattering

---

## What phenomenon is primarily responsible for the blue color of the sky: Core Concept

![What phenomenon is primarily responsible for the blue color of the sky Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/539b7dc85d26457a10f6e24715f0b782/800/300)

The correct answer is 'c' because Rayleigh scattering is the phenomenon where light is scattered by particles much smaller than the wavelength of the light\. This scattering is more effective for short wavelengths, such as blue and violet light, hence the sky's blue color\.

## Understanding: Would the sky still appear blue if the Earth had no atmosphere

![Would the sky still appear blue if the Earth had no atmosphere](https://picsum.photos/seed/e7251e012c80a1b85fb5c5f4985b6894/800/300)

**Question:** Would the sky still appear blue if the Earth had no atmosphere?

**Correct Answer:** No, because the blue color is due to the atmosphere scattering sunlight

---

![Would the sky still appear blue if the Earth had no atmosphere Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/001338d99573c90924702442f5dd2180/800/300)
