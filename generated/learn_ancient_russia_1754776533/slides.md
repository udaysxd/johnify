## Introduction to ancient russia
- **Ancient Russia** refers to the early East Slavic state of **Kievan Rus'** (9th–13th centuries), the foundation of modern Russia, Ukraine, and Belarus.
- It’s **historically significant** as the origin of Slavic culture, Orthodox Christianity, and early legal codes like the **Russkaya Pravda**.
- **Did you know?** At its height, Kievan Rus' was one of Europe’s **largest and wealthiest states**, with trade routes stretching from Scandinavia to Byzantium.

## Slide 2
- **The Kievan Rus'** - This was the first organized state in ancient Russia, founded by Viking traders (the **Varangians**) in the 9th century. It matters because it laid the foundation for Russian culture, religion, and political structure.
- **Adoption of Orthodox Christianity** - In 988, Prince **Vladimir the Great** converted Kievan Rus' to Orthodox Christianity, aligning it with the Byzantine Empire. This decision shaped Russia's religious identity and cultural ties to Europe.
- **The Mongol Invasion (13th century)** - The **Mongols** conquered Kievan Rus', leading to 200 years of domination (the **Golden Horde**). This period weakened the Rus' but also spurred the rise of Moscow as a future power center.
