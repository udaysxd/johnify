## Hotel California
- Released in 1976 on the Eagles' album of the same name, it reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1977.
- The song is approximately 6 minutes and 30 seconds long, featuring an iconic dual guitar solo that lasts for over 2 minutes.
- Lyrically, it tells a surreal narrative of a traveler checking into a luxurious but inescapable hotel, widely interpreted as a metaphor for the excesses of the California lifestyle and the music industry.
- It was recorded in Miami and Los Angeles using a 12-string guitar for the main riff and features Don Henley on lead vocals.
- The track has won two Grammy Awards: Record of the Year (1977) and, for its live version, Best Rock Performance by a Duo or Group with Vocal (1994).

- The lyrics are widely interpreted as a critique of the excess and hedonism of the American music industry and the California lifestyle.
