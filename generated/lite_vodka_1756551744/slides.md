## Vodka
- **Primary Ingredients:** Traditionally made from fermented grains like wheat or rye, or potatoes; modern versions can use corn, grapes, or even sugar beets.
- **Distillation & Proof:** Distilled at a high proof (often 190 or above) to achieve neutrality; typically bottled at 80 proof (40% alcohol by volume) in the US.
- **Filtration Process:** Commonly filtered through charcoal to remove impurities and achieve a clean, smooth profile with minimal flavor or aroma.
- **Serving Temperature:** Best served chilled, between 32–39°F (0–4°C), to enhance its crispness and slightly viscous mouthfeel.
- **Global Production Leaders:** Russia and Poland are historic producers; the European Union legally recognizes vodka only from potatoes or cereal grains.

## Core Ideas
- Vodka is legally defined as a neutral spirit distilled to 190+ proof and bottled at 40% ABV (80 proof) or higher.
- The primary base ingredients are grains (like wheat or rye) or potatoes, with some modern versions using corn, grapes, or sugar.
- A key production step is charcoal filtration, often through birch charcoal, to remove impurities and create a smooth, clean profile.
- Unlike aged spirits, premium vodka's value comes from distillation purity and water source quality, not barrel aging.
- It serves as the foundation for countless cocktails, from the classic Martini (gin substitute) and Moscow Mule to the Cosmopolitan.

## How To Learn
- **Taste blind**: Sample 3 vodkas side-by-side to identify differences in smoothness and character.
- **Chill to -18°C**: Serve vodka at this temperature to suppress ethanol burn and enhance texture.
- **Use a proper glass**: A tulip-shaped nosing glass concentrates aromas for better evaluation.
- **Add a splash of water**: A few drops can open up the spirit, releasing subtle floral or grain notes.
- **Note the finish**: A high-quality vodka should have a clean, pleasant aftertaste lasting 15-30 seconds.

## Examples
- **Production Volume**: Standard 750ml bottle requires ~2.2kg of grain or potatoes.
- **ABV Regulation**: Must be a minimum of 37.5% alcohol by volume in the EU and USA.
- **Leading Producers**: Russia, Poland, and Sweden are top exporters; Smirnoff is the world's best-selling brand.
- **Serving Temperature**: Best enjoyed chilled between 0-4°C (32-39°F) to suppress ethanol taste.
- **Classic Cocktails**: Key ingredient in Moscow Mule (45ml vodka, 120ml ginger beer), Cosmopolitan (45ml vodka, 15ml triple sec).

## Summary
- Vodka is legally defined as a neutral spirit distilled at or above 190 proof and bottled at no less than 80 proof in the US.
- It is traditionally made from fermented grains (e.g., wheat, rye) or potatoes, though modern producers also use corn, grapes, or sugar beets.
- The spirit is characterized by its neutrality; it is often filtered through charcoal to remove impurities and achieve a clean, crisp profile.
- Popular serving methods include chilled shots, in classic cocktails like the Moscow Mule (1.5 oz vodka, 0.5 oz lime, ginger beer), or as the base for a Martini.
- Leading global brands by volume include Smirnoff, Absolut, and Grey Goose, with the market valued at over $45 billion USD.
