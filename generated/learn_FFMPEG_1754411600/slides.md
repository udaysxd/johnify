## Introduction to FFMPEG
- **FFMPEG** is a **free, open-source** multimedia framework used for **recording, converting, and streaming** audio and video in various formats.
- It is **essential for developers and media professionals** because it supports nearly all codecs and formats, enabling seamless multimedia processing.
- **Fun fact:** FFMPEG is used by major platforms like YouTube, VLC, and Blender, processing **millions of hours** of video daily.

## Slide 2
- **Multimedia Processing**: FFMPEG is a **powerful toolkit** for handling audio, video, and other multimedia files. It matters because it enables **format conversion, editing, and streaming** in a single command-line tool.
- **Codec & Container Flexibility**: FFMPEG supports **hundreds of codecs and containers**, allowing seamless conversion between formats. This matters because it ensures compatibility across devices and platforms without losing quality.
- **Stream Manipulation**: FFMPEG can **split, merge, or filter** audio/video streams independently. This matters for advanced workflows like extracting audio, adding subtitles, or applying effects without re-encoding.

## Examples of FFMPEG
- **Video Streaming Conversion**: FFMPEG can **transcode live streams** from RTMP to HLS format, enabling compatibility with modern web players. This is critical for **real-time broadcasting** on platforms like Twitch or YouTube.
- **Batch Video Processing**: FFMPEG can **automate resizing, cropping, or compressing** hundreds of videos at once using scripts. This saves hours of manual work for **content creators and archivists**.
- **Audio Extraction & Normalization**: FFMPEG can **strip audio from video files** and adjust volume levels to a consistent standard. Podcasters and video editors rely on this for **clean, uniform soundtracks**.
