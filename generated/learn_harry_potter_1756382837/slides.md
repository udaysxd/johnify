## Harry Potter
- **7 Books in the Series**: The main story is told across seven novels, beginning with *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone* (1997) and concluding with *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows* (2007).
- **3 Unforgivable Curses**: The use of the Imperius Curse, Cruciatus Curse, or Killing Curse on another human will result in a life sentence in Azkaban prison.
- **4 Hogwarts Houses**: Students are Sorted into Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, or Slytherin based on their predominant traits: bravery, loyalty, intelligence, or ambition.
- **Horcrux Destruction Process**: To destroy a Horcrux, the object must be damaged beyond any magical repair, typically using basilisk venom, Fiendfyre, or the Sword of Gryffindor.
- **Wandlore Core Materials**: A wand's core is crucial to its power and allegiance; common cores include phoenix feather, dragon heartstring, and unicorn hair.

## Core Ideas
- **The Trace Charm**: Automatically activates on underage wizards, detecting magic performed outside school; breaches result in Ministry warnings or expulsion.
- **Wandlore Principles**: Wands choose the wizard (14,000 possible wood-core combinations); the Elder Wand requires mastery by defeat, not inheritance.
- **Horcrux Creation**: Requires murder to fracture the soul, followed by a spell to encase the fragment in an object (e.g., Tom Riddle's diary, Helga Hufflepuff's cup).
- **Pensieve Memory Viewing**: Memories can be reviewed, altered (e.g., Slughorn’s modified memory), or extracted via wand-tip to temple; stored in crystalline vials.
- **Portkey Travel**: Objects enchanted to transport users at predetermined times (e.g., Triwizard Cup portkey activated upon touch at 10:16 PM, June 24th, 1995).

## How To Learn
- **Practice wand movements daily**: Master the 10 core spells from Year 1, like Wingardium Leviosa, by repeating the swish-and-flick motion 20 times each session.
- **Brew potions with precision**: Follow recipes exactly, measuring ingredients like 5 drops of hellebore infusion and stirring 3 times clockwise to avoid explosions.
- **Read assigned textbooks thoroughly**: Study *The Standard Book of Spells, Grade 2* cover-to-cover, focusing on chapters 4–7 for midterm exams.
- **Join a study group**: Form a group of 4–6 peers to quiz each other on 15 defensive spells or debate the properties of 3 Unforgivable Curses.
- **Visit the library weekly**: Use the Restricted Section (with permission) to research advanced topics like Animagi registration or Patronus charm variations.

## Examples
- **Spellcasting Precision**: Wand movements must be exact; mispronouncing 'Wingardium Leviosa' (win-GAR-dee-um levi-O-sa) causes failure.
- **Potion Brewing Steps**: For Polyjuice Potion, stew lacewing flies 21 days, add fluxweed picked full moon, and stir 7 times counterclockwise.
- **Quidditch Scoring**: Each goal through hoops scores 10 points; catching Golden Snitch ends match and awards 150 points.
- **House Point System**: Awards/deductions given by professors (e.g., +50 for defeating troll, -20 for out-of-bounds night roaming).
- **Magical Creature Handling**: Bow to hippogriffs first; maintain eye contact until they return gesture to avoid attack.
- **Patronus Charm Requirement**: Focus on happiest memory (e.g., Harry’s first Quidditch win) while incanting 'Expecto Patronum'.

## Summary
- The series spans 7 books, 4,224 pages total, with the final installment selling 11 million copies in its first 24 hours.
- The core magical rule is Gamp's Law of Elemental Transfiguration, which has 5 Principal Exceptions, most notably that food cannot be conjured from nothing.
- A witch or wizard's magical power is channeled through a wand; the core must be from a magical creature (e.g., phoenix feather, dragon heartstring, unicorn hair).
- The primary antagonist is Lord Voldemort, who split his soul into 7 Horcruxes to achieve immortality, all of which had to be destroyed to defeat him.
- The three Unforgivable Curses (Avada Kedavra, Imperius, Cruciatus) are illegal to cast on another human and carry a mandatory life sentence in Azkaban prison.
