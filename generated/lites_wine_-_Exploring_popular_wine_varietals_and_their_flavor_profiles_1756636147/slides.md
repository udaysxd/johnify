## Introduction to Wine Varietals
- Grapes are the soul of wine.
- Varietals refer to specific grape types.
- Each varietal has unique flavors & aromas.
- Understanding varietals enhances your wine experience.

<!-- NARRATION: Welcome to our journey into the exciting world of wine! Today, we'll explore some popular wine grapes, known as varietals, and discover the delightful flavors and aromas they offer. Understanding these can truly elevate your appreciation for wine. -->

---

## Sauvignon Blanc
- Crisp and refreshing white wine.
- Key flavors: grapefruit, gooseberry, passionfruit.
- Often has herbal notes like grass or bell pepper.
- Acidity is typically high, making it food-friendly.

<!-- NARRATION: First up, we have Sauvignon Blanc, a wonderfully zesty white wine perfect for warm days. It's celebrated for its vibrant notes of citrus and tropical fruits, often complemented by intriguing herbal undertones. -->

---

## Chardonnay
- Versatile white wine, from crisp to full-bodied.
- Unoaked: green apple, citrus, mineral notes.
- Oaked: butter, vanilla, toast, tropical fruit.
- Can be aged, developing complex aromas.

<!-- NARRATION: Next, let's talk about Chardonnay, a white wine that can be as diverse as its winemaking. Depending on whether it's aged in oak, you can experience anything from bright apple and citrus to creamy butter and vanilla notes. -->

---

## Merlot
- Smooth and medium-bodied red wine.
- Flavors: plum, black cherry, chocolate.
- Softer tannins than Cabernet Sauvignon.
- Often used in blends for its fruitiness.

<!-- NARRATION: Moving to reds, Merlot is a fantastic choice for those who enjoy a smoother drinking experience. Expect luscious dark fruit flavors like plum and cherry, often with a hint of chocolate. -->

---

## Cabernet Sauvignon
- Full-bodied and robust red wine.
- Key flavors: blackcurrant, cedar, tobacco, mint.
- High tannins provide structure and aging potential.
- Often paired with rich, savory dishes.

<!-- NARRATION: Finally, we arrive at Cabernet Sauvignon, a king among red wines known for its bold character. It boasts rich blackcurrant flavors and a structure that makes it ideal for cellaring and pairing with hearty meals. -->
