## How is Hungarian langos typically cooked: Core Concept

![How is Hungarian langos typically cooked Concept](https://picsum.photos/800/300?random=1752867131)

Hungarian langos is traditionally fried in oil, which gives it its characteristic crispy exterior and soft interior\. Baking, grilling, or steaming would not achieve the same texture\.
