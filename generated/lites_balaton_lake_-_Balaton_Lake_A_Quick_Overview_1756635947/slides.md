## Geography & Size
- Length: 77 km (48 miles)
- Average width: 1.5 km (0.9 miles)
- Shallow water: average depth 3.2 meters
- Water temperature: up to 26°C in summer
- Fed by River Zala

<!-- NARRATION: Balaton stretches an impressive 77 kilometers in length, though it's quite narrow on average. Its shallow waters are perfect for swimming and warm up nicely in the summer months. -->
