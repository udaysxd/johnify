## Introduction to why are women not safe in india and mexico?
- **Why are women not safe in India and Mexico?** Women in both countries face **high rates of gender-based violence**, including harassment, assault, and femicide, due to deep-rooted **patriarchal norms** and weak legal enforcement.
- **Why is this important?** Gender violence **threatens women's rights**, limits their freedom, and harms societal progress—making it a **global human rights issue**.
- **Did you know?** In India, a woman reports a **rape every 16 minutes**, while Mexico records **10 femicides a day**, highlighting the **urgent need for change**.
