## Understanding: Which event marked the beginning of the systematic, state\-sponsored persecution of Jews in Nazi Germany

![Which event marked the beginning of the systematic, state\-sponsored persecution of Jews in Nazi Germany](https://picsum.photos/seed/577878567e92a99dcdfa56d8c1e16e87/800/300)

**Question:** Which event marked the beginning of the systematic, state\-sponsored persecution of Jews in Nazi Germany?

**Correct Answer:** The passing of the Nuremberg Laws

---

## Which event marked the beginning of the systematic, state\-sponsored persecution of Jews in Nazi Germany: Core Concept

![Which event marked the beginning of the systematic, state\-sponsored persecution of Jews in Nazi Germany Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/f3f0eca5e6cbc615db56e42a89bdab33/800/300)

The Nuremberg Laws, enacted in 1935, were the first major step in the legal persecution of Jews in Nazi Germany\. These laws stripped Jews of their citizenship and prohibited marriage or sexual relations between Jews and non\-Jews, laying the groundwork for further atrocities\.

*Key points to remember:*
- The Nuremberg Laws, enacted in 1935, were the first major step in the legal persecution of Jews in Nazi Germany\.
- These laws stripped Jews of their citizenship and prohibited marriage or sexual relations between Jews and non\-Jews, laying the groundwork for further atrocities\.
- This concept is fundamental to understanding the topic.
