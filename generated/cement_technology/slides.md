## What is the primary raw material used in the production of Portland cement: Core Concept

![What is the primary raw material used in the production of Portland cement Concept](file://C:\johnify\image_cache\d7e17fba828d8f84c9963920c793be10.jpg)

Limestone is the primary raw material used in the production of Portland cement because it provides the necessary calcium carbonate, which is a key component in the cement\-making process\. Sand and clay are also used but in smaller quantities, and gypsum is added to control the setting time of cement\.

## Understanding: Which of the following compounds is responsible for the initial setting of cement

![Which of the following compounds is responsible for the initial setting of cement](file://C:\johnify\image_cache\bc22e31e07181291f955b46ceb592d53.jpg)

**Question:** Which of the following compounds is responsible for the initial setting of cement?

**Correct Answer:** Tricalcium aluminate \(C3A\)

---

## Which of the following compounds is responsible for the initial setting of cement: Core Concept

![Which of the following compounds is responsible for the initial setting of cement Concept](file://C:\johnify\image_cache\78c0b91bb571769734f4c1c2e6b4d277.jpg)

Tricalcium aluminate \(C3A\) is responsible for the initial setting of cement due to its rapid reaction with water\. Tricalcium silicate \(C3S\) and dicalcium silicate \(C2S\) contribute to the strength of the cement, while tetracalcium aluminoferrite \(C4AF\) has a minor role in the setting and hardening process\.

## Understanding: What is the purpose of adding gypsum to cement

![What is the purpose of adding gypsum to cement](file://C:\johnify\image_cache\603ded8f8729626b2b96fcab84339526.jpg)

**Question:** What is the purpose of adding gypsum to cement?

**Correct Answer:** To control the setting time of cement

---

## What is the purpose of adding gypsum to cement: Core Concept

![What is the purpose of adding gypsum to cement Concept](file://C:\johnify\image_cache\01318bdb7993e39f5a22fa7aeeacf1bf.jpg)

Gypsum is added to cement to control the setting time by slowing down the hydration process of tricalcium aluminate \(C3A\), which would otherwise cause the cement to set too quickly\. It does not significantly increase the strength, improve the color, or reduce the cost of cement production\.
