## Austria: A Snapshot
- Central European nation
- Known for Alps & classical music
- Capital: Vienna
- Official language: German
- Currency: Euro

## Majestic Alps & Landscapes
- Dominant mountain range: the Alps
- Offers stunning scenery
- Popular for skiing & hiking
- Großglockner: highest peak
- Picturesque valleys & lakes

## Vienna: Imperial Grandeur
- Cultural and political heart
- Former Habsburg empire center
- Must-see: Schönbrunn Palace
- St. Stephen's Cathedral iconic
- World-renowned for coffee houses

<!-- NARRATION: Vienna, the capital city, is a testament to Austria's imperial past, once serving as the center of the vast Habsburg Empire. Visitors are captivated by stunning palaces like Schönbrunn and iconic landmarks such as St. Stephen's Cathedral. -->

## Music & Arts Heritage
- Birthplace of classical giants
- Mozart, Beethoven, Strauss legacies
- Vienna State Opera famous
- Numerous museums & galleries
- Rich tradition in art and architecture

<!-- NARRATION: Austria boasts an unparalleled musical heritage, being the homeland of legendary composers like Mozart and Beethoven. Its cities, especially Vienna, resonate with the echoes of classical music, featuring world-class opera houses and museums. -->

- Festivals celebrate traditions
- Enjoyed for its quality of life
