## Osho On Life
- Life is not a problem to be solved but a mystery to be lived, requiring no logical solution but total participation in each moment.
- Embrace "Zorba the Buddha" by celebrating the physical (like dancing 30 minutes daily) while cultivating meditative awareness.
- Drop conditionings and ideologies; for example, spend 1 hour in nature weekly observing without judgment to reconnect with reality.
- Understand that death is not the opposite of life but part of its cycle—meditate on your mortality for 5 minutes each day to live more intensely.
- Avoid seeking permanent happiness; instead, accept that life is a river of change, with sadness, joy, and anger each lasting 2-3 days naturally.
- Practice "witnessing" by observing your thoughts for 10-minute sessions twice daily to detach from the mind and experience consciousness directly.

## Core Ideas
- Life is a river, not a destination; the goal is to flow, not to arrive somewhere.
- Meditation is the master key: sit silently for 20 minutes daily to witness thoughts without judgment.
- Drop all conditionings imposed by society, religion, and parents to discover your authentic self.
- Celebrate existence itself; find joy not in achievements, but in the ordinary—a cup of tea, a walk.
- Embrace the polarities: say a total 'yes' to both pain and pleasure, seeing them as two sides of life.
- Live in the present; the past is a dead memory, the future a fantasy—only now is real.

## How To Learn
- **Unlearn first**: Shed all conditioning from parents, society, and religion to create space for true learning, as preconceived notions block receptivity.
- **Learn through experience, not intellect**: Engage directly with life—touch, feel, and immerse yourself; for example, don't just read about love, be in love to understand its depths.
- **Become a child again**: Embrace curiosity, wonder, and non-judgmental observation; approach each moment as if seeing it for the first time, without past biases.
- **Meditate daily**: Sit silently for at least 20 minutes, witnessing thoughts without attachment to dissolve the ego, which obstructs learning by claiming it already knows.
- **Doubt everything**: Question all teachings, including Osho's; use doubt as a tool to inquire deeply until you arrive at your own truth, not borrowed beliefs.

## Examples
- **Life is not a problem to be solved, but a mystery to be lived** – Osho emphasizes that approaching existence with curiosity rather than analysis leads to deeper fulfillment.  
- **Meditation as a tool for awareness** – He recommends daily 20-minute dynamic meditation sessions to dissolve mental conditioning and connect with the present moment.  
- **Celebrate individuality, reject conformity** – Osho argues that societal structures often suppress authentic expression, and encourages discovering one’s unique path without guilt.  
- **Embrace contradictions; life is paradoxical** – For example, he teaches that joy and sorrow are inseparable—accepting both expands emotional capacity.  
- **Live intensely in the ‘now’** – Practices like conscious breathing or mindful walking anchor attention to the immediate experience, reducing attachment to past/future.

## Summary
- Life is not a problem to be solved but a mystery to be lived, requiring surrender to the present moment without seeking logical answers
- Authentic living demands rebellion against all conditioning—religious, societal, and educational—to discover one’s true nature beyond imposed ideologies
- Celebrate existence intensely through all opposites: pain/pleasure, success/failure, life/death, seeing each as a facet of the same cosmic play
- Meditation is the core methodology: 1 hour daily of witnessing thoughts without judgment to dissolve the ego and access inner silence
- Responsibility means owning your actions, feelings, and reality completely, rejecting all blame toward parents, society, or circumstances
- Love authentically without attachment—experience relationships as joyful sharing of energy rather than contracts of expectation or dependency
