## Adele Hello Song Lyrics
- Written in A-flat major with a tempo of 79 BPM, featuring a prominent piano and string arrangement
- Verse-Chorus structure: Intro (0:00-0:15), Verse 1 (0:15-0:45), Pre-Chorus (0:45-1:00), Chorus (1:00-1:30), Verse 2 (1:30-2:00), Chorus (2:00-2:30), Bridge (2:30-3:15), Final Chorus (3:15-4:00), Outro (4:00-4:55)
- Key lyrical themes: Apology ("Hello from the other side"), regret ("I'm sorry for breaking your heart"), and unresolved past relationships
- Vocal technique: Uses chest voice for verses (e.g., "Hello, it's me") and powerful belting in choruses, reaching up to E♭5
- Repetitive anaphora with "Hello" starting 4 lines in the first verse and appearing 21 times throughout the song
- Grammy Award winner for Record of the Year, Song of the Year, and Best Pop Solo Performance (2017)

## Core Ideas
- **Lyrical Structure:** 32-line song divided into 4 verses and a repeating chorus, with no traditional bridge section.
- **Central Theme:** A direct, one-sided conversation expressing regret and longing for reconciliation after a long separation.
- **Key Lyrical Device:** Heavy use of anaphora with the repetition of "Hello from the..." to create a pleading, persistent tone.
- **Narrative Progression:** The story evolves from a simple "hello" to an admission of failed past attempts and a deep apology.
- **Emotional Arc:** Moves from nostalgic reflection ("We were younger and free") to raw, unresolved heartache ("I'm sorry for breaking your heart").

## How To Learn
- **Listen actively 3–5 times** without lyrics to identify repeated phrases like "Hello from the other side" and the emotional tone of Adele's delivery.
- **Break lyrics into thematic sections**: verse (storytelling), chorus (emotional peak), and bridge (reflection), noting how each builds the narrative.
- **Analyze specific vocabulary** such as "scar," "regret," or "million miles" to understand the song's themes of apology and distance.
- **Sing along with timed pauses** to practice pronunciation, especially challenging words like "anymore" or "rearrange," and mimic Adele's vocal style.
- **Compare live vs. studio versions** to hear how tempo, emphasis, or ad-libs (e.g., extended notes in "hello") change the song's impact.

## Examples
- **Verse 1 opening lines:** "Hello, it's me / I was wondering if after all these years you'd like to meet" – establishes the song's central theme of reaching out after a long period of silence.
- **Chorus lyric structure:** Repeats the word "Hello" four times consecutively ("Hello from the other side / I must've called a thousand times") to emphasize desperation and the one-sided nature of the communication.
- **Use of numbers for impact:** The line "I must've called a thousand times" uses hyperbole to convey the immense, repeated effort of trying to connect, making the emotion feel vast and overwhelming.
- **Bridge contrast:** Shifts from reflection to a direct, painful admission with "But it don't matter, it clearly doesn't tear you apart anymore," highlighting the one-sided healing and unresolved feelings.
