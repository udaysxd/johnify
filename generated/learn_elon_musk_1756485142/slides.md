## Elon Musk
- Founded SpaceX in 2002 with the goal of reducing space transportation costs and enabling Mars colonization; Falcon 9 rocket achieves a 91% success rate over 300 launches as of 2024.
- Co-founded and leads Tesla, Inc., accelerating the world's transition to sustainable energy; Tesla Model 3 became the world's best-selling EV in 2023 with over 1.2 million units sold.
- Proposed the Hyperloop concept in a 2013 white paper, a high-speed transportation system in a low-pressure tube; initiated the Hyperloop Pod Competition from 2015–2019 to spur development.
- Acquired Twitter for approximately $44 billion in October 2022 and rebranded it as X, aiming to create an "everything app" with features like peer-to-peer payments and long-form content.

## Core Ideas
- Founded or co-founded 6 major companies: Zip2 (1995), X.com/PayPal (1999), SpaceX (2002), Tesla (2004), SolarCity (2006), Neuralink (2016), and The Boring Co. (2016).
- SpaceX's Falcon 9 rocket was the first orbital-class rocket to achieve reflight and landing (2015), reducing launch costs by ~30%.
- Tesla's "Master Plan" (2006) outlined a 3-step strategy: build a sports car (Roadster), use that money for an affordable car (Model S/3), and use *that* money for an even more affordable car.
- Advocates for a "multi-planetary species" vision, with SpaceX targeting an uncrewed Mars mission as early as 2028.
- Employs a first-principles reasoning approach, breaking down problems to their fundamental truths to engineer novel solutions.

## How To Learn
- Read 2+ hours daily across diverse fields; Musk read 10+ hours daily as a child.
- Apply first principles thinking: break problems into fundamental truths, then rebuild (e.g., questioning rocket cost assumptions).
- Seek negative feedback actively; iterate based on critiques, as done with early SpaceX and Tesla prototypes.
- Transfer knowledge between industries; apply software update principles from tech to car manufacturing.
- Learn by doing: hands-on involvement in engineering, like personally reviewing and correcting rocket designs.

## Examples
- **Tesla Model 3**: Over 1.8 million units sold globally as of 2023, making it the world's best-selling electric vehicle.
- **SpaceX Falcon 9**: First orbital-class rocket capable of reflight, with over 270 launches and 220 landings to date.
- **Neuralink's N1 Implant**: A brain-computer interface with 1,024 electrodes, aiming to restore mobility and vision.
- **The Boring Company's LVCC Loop**: A 1.7-mile tunnel system in Las Vegas that reduces travel time from 45 minutes to 2 minutes.
- **Solar Roof Tiles**: Tesla's integrated solar solution generating up to 15 kW per day for an average home.
