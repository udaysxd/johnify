## What was the main purpose of the Berlin Wall: Core Concept

![What was the main purpose of the Berlin Wall Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/e2bb78c26c3357be31aabf65351a6f3e/800/300)

The Berlin Wall was built to separate East and West Berlin, preventing the mass emigration and defection that had marked East Germany and the communist Eastern Bloc during the post\-World War II period\. It was not for protection, tourism, or marking borders with other countries\.

## When was the Berlin Wall constructed: Core Concept

![When was the Berlin Wall constructed Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/477fb874af9dfcb10e4449e75831fbd6/800/300)
