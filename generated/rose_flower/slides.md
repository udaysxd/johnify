## What is the scientific name for the rose: Core Concept

![What is the scientific name for the rose Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/601d309d45bbf9856e4ee55c241008a0/800/300)

The scientific name for the rose is 'Rosa'\. 'Tulipa' refers to tulips, 'Daisy' is a common name for flowers in the Asteraceae family, and 'Lilium' refers to lilies\.

*Key points to remember:*

- 'Tulipa' refers to tulips, 'Daisy' is a common name for flowers in the Asteraceae family, and 'Lilium' refers to lilies\.
