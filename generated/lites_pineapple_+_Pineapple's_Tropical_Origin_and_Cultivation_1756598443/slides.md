## The Tropical Treasure: Pineapple's Origins
- Native to South America, specifically the Paraná River basin.
- First cultivated by indigenous peoples, likely the Tupi-Guarani.
- Discovered by Christopher Columbus in 1493 during his second voyage.
- Spread rapidly through tropical regions worldwide by European explorers.

## From Seed to Sweetness: Pineapple Cultivation
- A bromeliad, growing from a central crown or slips.
- Requires warm temperatures, high humidity, and plenty of sunlight.
- Grown in tropical and subtropical climates globally.
- Primary growing regions: Costa Rica, Philippines, Brazil, Thailand.

## Growing Conditions: What Pineapples Need
- Optimal temperature range: 23-30°C (73-86°F).
- Well-drained, slightly acidic soil (pH 5.5-6.5).
- Requires adequate rainfall or irrigation.
- Can be grown hydroponically in controlled environments.

## The Pineapple Plant: More Than Just Fruit
- Produces a single fruit per plant after 18-24 months.
- Leaves are fibrous and historically used for textiles (Piña cloth).
- The plant itself is an ornamental beauty.
- Bromelain enzyme in fruit and stem has various uses.

## Global Journey: Pineapple's Widespread Reach
- Introduced to Europe in the 17th century.
- Became a symbol of hospitality and luxury.
- Commercial cultivation boomed in the 20th century.
- Now a major commodity crop, enjoyed worldwide.
