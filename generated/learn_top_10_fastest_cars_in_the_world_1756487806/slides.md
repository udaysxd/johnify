## Top 10 Fastest Cars In The World
*   **1. SSC Tuatara:** Holds the verified production car record at 282.9 mph (455.3 km/h), achieved on a closed road in Nevada, USA.
*   **2. Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut:** Boasts a theoretical top speed of over 330 mph (531 km/h), enabled by a 1600 hp twin-turbo V8 and a drag coefficient of just 0.278 Cd.
*   **3. Hennessey Venom F5:** Targets 311+ mph (500+ km/h) with its 6.6L 'Fury' twin-turbo V8 producing 1817 hp and an ultra-light 1360 kg carbon fiber monocoque.
*   **4. Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+:** First hypercar to break 300 mph, officially recorded at 304.773 mph (490.484 km/h), powered by a 1578 hp quad-turbo W16 engine.
*   **5. Rimac Nevera:** The fastest accelerating electric hypercar, with a top speed of 258 mph (412 km/h) and 1914 hp from its four independent electric motors.
