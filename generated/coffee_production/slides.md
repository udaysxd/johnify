## What is the most common species of coffee plant cultivated for coffee production: Core Concept

![What is the most common species of coffee plant cultivated for coffee production Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/ccbcfcc4c52b5143eb6b880b993058c5/800/300)

Coffea arabica is the most common species of coffee plant cultivated for coffee production, accounting for about 60\-70% of global coffee production\. It is prized for its smooth, complex flavor profiles\. Coffea canephora \(Robusta\) is the second most common, known for its stronger, more bitter taste and higher caffeine content\.

- Coffea canephora \(Robusta\) is the second most common, known for its stronger, more bitter taste and higher caffeine content\.

## Understanding: What is the process called where coffee cherries are dried in the sun before the beans are removed

![What is the process called where coffee cherries are dried in the sun before the beans are removed](https://picsum.photos/seed/2c50544d57adb9fc70ff46dabbf412dc/800/300)

**Question:** What is the process called where coffee cherries are dried in the sun before the beans are removed?

**Correct Answer:** Dry processing

---

## What is the process called where coffee cherries are dried in the sun before the beans are removed: Core Concept

![What is the process called where coffee cherries are dried in the sun before the beans are removed Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/6b2c464a7663bb937fe7c4a2b1f833a2/800/300)

Dry processing, also known as natural processing, involves drying the whole coffee cherries in the sun before removing the beans\. This method is traditional and often used in regions with limited water resources\. Wet processing involves removing the pulp from the cherry before drying, semi\-dry is a hybrid method, and honey processing leaves some of the mucilage on the bean during drying\.

*Key points to remember:*
- Dry processing, also known as natural processing, involves drying the whole coffee cherries in the sun before removing the beans\.
- This method is traditional and often used in regions with limited water resources\.
- Wet processing involves removing the pulp from the cherry before drying, semi\-dry is a hybrid method, and honey processing leaves some of the mucilage on the bean during drying\.
