## What was the primary writing material used in ancient Egypt: Core Concept

![What was the primary writing material used in ancient Egypt Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/5a8977d386eed435b7cb2276c4bbbbf8/800/300)

Papyrus, made from the pith of the papyrus plant, was the primary writing material in ancient Egypt\. Clay tablets were used in Mesopotamia, while stone and wood were used for inscriptions and carvings but not as the primary writing material\.

*Key points to remember:*
- Papyrus, made from the pith of the papyrus plant, was the primary writing material in ancient Egypt\.
- Clay tablets were used in Mesopotamia, while stone and wood were used for inscriptions and carvings but not as the primary writing material\.
- This concept is fundamental to understanding the topic.

## Who was the first pharaoh of ancient Egypt: Core Concept

![Who was the first pharaoh of ancient Egypt Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/7e77e5ce3759be2bc67cbf9f8a8cc772/800/300)

Narmer is considered the first pharaoh to have unified Upper and Lower Egypt, marking the beginning of the dynastic period of ancient Egypt\. Tutankhamun, Ramses II, and Cleopatra were all rulers of Egypt but came much later in its history\.

*Key points to remember:*
- Narmer is considered the first pharaoh to have unified Upper and Lower Egypt, marking the beginning of the dynastic period of ancient Egypt\.
