# Key Concepts for you

Here are the concepts you might want to review:

---

## Which album by Coldplay was released in 2000?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** Parachutes


**Explanation:** Parachutes is Coldplay's debut studio album, released in 2000\. 'A Rush of Blood to the Head' came out in 2002, 'X&Y' in 2005, and 'Viva la Vida or Death and All His Friends' in 2008\.

---

## What is the name of Coldplay's lead vocalist?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** Chris Martin


**Explanation:** Chris Martin is the lead vocalist and co\-founder of Coldplay\. Jonny Buckland is the lead guitarist, Guy Berryman is the bassist, and Will Champion is the drummer\.

---

## Which Coldplay song won the Grammy Award for Song of the Year in 2009?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** Viva la Vida


**Explanation:** 'Viva la Vida' won the Grammy Award for Song of the Year in 2009\. 'Clocks' won Record of the Year in 2004, while 'Yellow' and 'The Scientist' were nominated for various awards but did not win in this category\.
