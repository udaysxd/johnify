## Introduction to ancient india
- **Ancient India** refers to the **Indian subcontinent's history** from around **3300 BCE to 500 CE**, encompassing major civilizations like the **Indus Valley** and empires like the **Maurya and Gupta**.
- It is **important** for its contributions to **mathematics, medicine, philosophy, and art**, which shaped global knowledge and culture.
- **Interesting fact**: Ancient Indians invented the **concept of zero** and the **decimal system**, revolutionizing mathematics worldwide.

## Slide 2
- **Indus Valley Civilization**: One of the world's **earliest urban civilizations** (2600–1900 BCE), known for advanced city planning, drainage systems, and trade. It matters because it shows how **organized and innovative** ancient Indian societies were.
- **Vedic Period**: Marked by the composition of the **Vedas**, sacred texts that shaped Hinduism, philosophy, and social structures like the **caste system**. This period matters because it laid the foundation for **Indian culture and religion**.
- **Maurya Empire**: Founded by **Chandragupta Maurya** (322 BCE), it was India's first major empire, famous for **Ashoka's spread of Buddhism**. It matters because it unified much of India and promoted **peace and tolerance**.
