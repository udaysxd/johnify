## What is Crypto Staking?
- Lock crypto assets
- Earn rewards
- Support network

<!-- NARRATION: Staking is like earning interest on your crypto. You lock up your digital assets to help secure a blockchain network and, in return, you get rewarded. -->

## How Does It Work?
- Proof-of-Stake
- Validate transactions
- Mint new coins

<!-- NARRATION: It typically works on a Proof-of-Stake system. By staking, you become a validator, helping to verify transactions and create new blocks on the blockchain. -->
