## What is Green Tea?
- Made from Camellia sinensis plant leaves.
- Undergoes minimal oxidation during processing.
- Steamed or pan-fired to halt oxidation.
- Preserves beneficial compounds, especially catechins.
- Popular worldwide for its unique flavor.

<!-- NARRATION: Green tea comes from the leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant, but what makes it special is how it's processed. It's minimally oxidized, meaning its natural compounds are well-preserved, offering a unique taste and health advantages. -->

---

## Key Health Benefits: Antioxidants
- Rich in polyphenols, particularly catechins.
- Epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG) is the most potent.
- Antioxidants combat free radicals in the body.
- Free radicals can damage cells and contribute to disease.
- May help protect against chronic illnesses.

<!-- NARRATION: One of green tea's biggest health boosts comes from its high antioxidant content, especially a powerful catechin called EGCG. These antioxidants fight harmful free radicals in your body, offering protection against cellular damage and chronic diseases. -->

## Heart Health & Green Tea
- May help improve cholesterol levels.
- Can reduce 'bad' LDL cholesterol.
- May help lower blood pressure.
- Contributes to improved blood vessel function.
- Regular consumption linked to a lower risk of heart disease.

<!-- NARRATION: Enjoying green tea can be great for your heart! It's known to help improve cholesterol profiles by lowering bad LDL cholesterol and may also contribute to lower blood pressure, supporting overall cardiovascular well-being. -->

## Brain Function & Mood
- Contains L-theanine, an amino acid.
- L-theanine promotes relaxation without drowsiness.
- Works synergistically with caffeine for alertness.
- May improve cognitive function and focus.
- Can enhance mood and reduce anxiety.

<!-- NARRATION: Need a mental boost? Green tea contains L-theanine, an amino acid that, along with caffeine, can enhance focus and alertness without the jitters. It's also linked to improved mood and reduced feelings of anxiety. -->

## Other Potential Benefits
