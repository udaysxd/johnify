## Introduction to ancient egypt
- **Ancient Egypt** was a **powerful civilization** that thrived along the Nile River for over **3,000 years**, known for its pyramids, pharaohs, and advanced culture.
- It’s **historically significant** for its contributions to writing (hieroglyphs), architecture, and early advancements in medicine and engineering.
- **Interesting fact**: The Great Pyramid of Giza, built around 2560 BCE, was the **tallest man-made structure** for over 3,800 years at 481 feet (146 meters).

## Slide 2
- **The Nile River**: The Nile was the **lifeblood of ancient Egypt**, providing water, fertile soil, and transportation. It mattered because it allowed agriculture to thrive, supporting the civilization's growth and stability.
- **Hieroglyphics**: Ancient Egyptians used **hieroglyphics**, a system of pictorial writing, to record history, religion, and daily life. This matters because it gives us insight into their culture, beliefs, and advancements.
- **Pyramids**: The pyramids were **monumental tombs** built for pharaohs, showcasing engineering and religious devotion. They matter because they reflect the Egyptians' architectural skill and their belief in the afterlife.

## Examples of ancient egypt
- **The Great Pyramid of Giza**
- Built as a tomb for Pharaoh **Khufu** around 2580–2560 BCE, it is the largest of the three pyramids at Giza.
- Its **precision engineering** and scale reflect the advanced skills and organizational power of ancient Egypt.
