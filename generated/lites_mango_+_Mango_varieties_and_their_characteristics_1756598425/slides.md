## The Marvelous Mango
- A tropical fruit loved worldwide.
- Rich in vitamins and antioxidants.
- Comes in hundreds of varieties.
- Sweet, tangy, and juicy.
- Enjoyed fresh, in smoothies, or desserts.

<!-- NARRATION: Welcome to the sweet world of mangoes! This tropical delight is famous for its amazing taste and health benefits, and there's a whole universe of varieties to explore. -->

---

## Alphonso: The King of Mangoes
- Highly prized for its rich, sweet flavor.
- Creamy texture, saffron-yellow flesh.
- Originates from India.
- Low fiber content.
- Excellent for pulp and juice.

<!-- NARRATION: Let's start with the 'King of Mangoes,' the Alphonso. It's renowned for its incredibly sweet, creamy texture and rich flavor, making it a top choice for many. -->

---

## Kent: A Smooth Operator
- Large fruit with a vibrant red and green skin.
- Smooth, non-fibrous, bright orange flesh.
- Sweet flavor with a slight tartness.
- Good shelf life.
- Popular in many international markets.

<!-- NARRATION: Next up is the Kent mango, a large fruit with beautiful skin and delightfully smooth, non-fibrous flesh. Its balanced sweet and tart flavor makes it a global favorite. -->

---

## Ataulfo (Honey Mango): Delicate Sweetness
- Small to medium size, kidney-shaped.
- Smooth, bright yellow skin.
- Buttery, fiberless flesh.
- Intensely sweet and mild flavor.
- Often called 'champagne' mangoes.

<!-- NARRATION: Discover the Ataulfo, also known as the Honey mango. These small, golden gems offer a buttery, fiberless texture and an intensely sweet, mild taste that's truly exquisite. -->

---

## Tommy Atkins: The Traveler
- Widely available and recognizable.
- Reddish-green skin with yellow and orange hues.
- Fibrous flesh with a mild, slightly tangy taste.
- Good shelf life and shipping durability.
- A common choice for everyday enjoyment.

<!-- NARRATION: Finally, we have the Tommy Atkins, a very common variety recognized by its colorful skin. While a bit more fibrous, its mild flavor and excellent durability make it a popular and accessible option. -->
