## Apple
- Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne; current market capitalization exceeds $3 trillion.
- Key products include iPhone (launched 2007, over 2.2 billion units sold), Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods.
- Operates services like the App Store (1.8+ million apps), Apple Music (over 100 million songs), and iCloud.
- Runs on proprietary operating systems: iOS, macOS, watchOS, and iPadOS, ensuring tight hardware-software integration.
- Committed to environmental goals: aim for 100% carbon neutral products and supply chain by 2030.

## How To Learn
- **Engage in active recall**: After studying, close your notes and write down everything you remember. Studies show this improves retention by up to 50% compared to passive re-reading.
- **Space out your practice**: Review material over multiple sessions (e.g., 3 sessions of 20 minutes) instead of one long cram. This spacing effect strengthens long-term memory.
- **Apply the Feynman Technique**: Teach the concept to someone else, or pretend to, using simple language. This exposes gaps in your understanding immediately.
- **Get specific feedback**: Instead of "good job," ask for targeted feedback on one specific area, like the structure of your argument or a particular calculation.
