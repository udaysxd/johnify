## Understanding Shirt Fit
- Shoulders: Seams should sit at the edge of your shoulders.
- Chest: No pulling or tightness when buttoned.
- Waist: A slight taper is ideal, not too boxy.
- Sleeve Length: Ends at the wrist bone, allowing for cuff visibility.
- Collar: Comfortable, with room for two fingers.

## Common Fit Types
- Classic/Regular Fit: Relaxed, comfortable, good for most body types.
- Slim Fit: Tapered through the waist and sleeves for a modern silhouette.
- Athletic Fit: Wider shoulders, narrower waist, designed for muscular builds.
- Relaxed Fit: Looser overall, offers maximum comfort and movement.

<!-- NARRATION: There are several common fit types available, each designed for different body shapes and style preferences. From classic relaxed to modern slim and athletic fits, finding your ideal cut is important. -->

---

## Shirt Collar Styles
- Spread Collar: Wider points, versatile for ties or open.
- Point/Classic Collar: Narrower points, traditional and formal.
- Button-Down Collar: Points button to the shirt, casual yet neat.
- Mandarin/Band Collar: Collarless, modern and minimalist.

<!-- NARRATION: The collar is a crucial detail that frames your face and influences the shirt's formality. Spread, point, button-down, and band collars offer distinct looks for various occasions. -->

---

## Sleeve and Cuff Options
- Short Sleeve: Casual, ideal for warmer weather.
- Long Sleeve: Classic, can be dressed up or down.
- Cuffs: Barrel (buttoned), French (cufflinks), Mitered (angled).

<!-- NARRATION: Sleeves and cuffs also contribute to a shirt's overall style and function. Whether you prefer short or long sleeves, and the type of cuff you choose, adds a personal touch. -->

---

## Choosing Your Style
- Consider the occasion: Formal, business casual, or casual.
- Body shape influences the best fit: Experiment to find what flatters you.
- Fabric and pattern play a role in formality.
- Ultimately, comfort and confidence are key!

<!-- NARRATION: When choosing your shirt style, remember to consider the event, your body shape, and the fabric. The best shirt is one that makes you feel comfortable and confident! -->
