## Introduction to ancient ghana
- **Ancient Ghana** was a powerful West African kingdom that thrived between **300–1200 CE**, known for its wealth in **gold and trade**.
- It’s historically significant as one of the **earliest major empires in Africa** and a key hub for the **trans-Saharan trade network**.
- An interesting fact: Ancient Ghana’s rulers were so rich that they **tied gold nuggets to their dogs’ collars** to showcase their wealth!

## Slide 2
- **Gold and Salt Trade** - Ancient Ghana grew wealthy by controlling the **trans-Saharan trade** of gold from the south and salt from the north. This mattered because it made Ghana a powerful economic hub in West Africa.
- **Strong Centralized Government** - The king of Ghana, called the **Ghana (or "Warrior King")**, ruled with strict authority and collected taxes on trade. This mattered because it ensured stability and prosperity for the empire.
- **Islamic Influence** - Ghana’s rulers allowed **Muslim merchants and scholars** to settle in cities, spreading Islam and literacy. This mattered because it connected Ghana to the wider Islamic world and boosted cultural exchange.
