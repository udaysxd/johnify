## How does the human brain's plasticity contribute to intelligence: Core Concept

![How does the human brain's plasticity contribute to intelligence Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/c7053afcbc2ba6e9803b8d5e29efd7bc/800/300)

Brain plasticity, or neuroplasticity, refers to the brain's ability to reorganize itself by forming new neural connections throughout life\. This adaptability allows humans to learn from experiences, acquire new skills, and recover from injuries, contributing significantly to intelligence\.

*Key points to remember:*
- Brain plasticity, or neuroplasticity, refers to the brain's ability to reorganize itself by forming new neural connections throughout life\.
