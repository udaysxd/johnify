# Slide 2

## 🌍 Introduction: What Are Phone Radiations?  
- **Definition**: Electromagnetic waves emitted by mobile devices.  
- **Types**:  
  - *Ionizing* (e.g., X-rays – high energy, harmful).  
  - *Non-ionizing* (e.g., phone signals – low energy, debated effects).  
- **Why Care?**  
  - We spend ~4+ hours/day on phones.  
  - Ongoing research on long-term health impacts.

---

# Slide 3

## 📡 How Do Phones Emit Radiation?  
- **Process**:  
  - Phones use radiofrequency (RF) waves to connect to towers/WiFi.  
  - Stronger signals = more radiation (e.g., weak reception areas).  
- **Example**:  
  - Streaming in a basement → phone works harder → higher RF emission.

---

# Slide 4

## ⚠️ Potential Health Concerns  
- **Debated Effects**:  
  - *Possible links*: Sleep disruption, headaches (WHO classifies RF as "possibly carcinogenic").  
  - *No conclusive proof*: More research needed.  
- **Example**:  
  - Sleeping with phone under pillow → increased exposure.

---

# Slide 5

## 📲 Reducing Exposure: Practical Tips  
- **Simple Habits**:  
  - Use speakerphone/earbuds for calls.  
  - Keep phone away from body (e.g., bag vs. pocket).  
  - Text instead of call when possible.  
- **Example**:  
  - Airplane mode at night = zero radiation.

---

# Slide 6

## 🔍 Myths vs. Facts  
- **Myth**: "5G is dangerously radioactive."  
  - *Fact*: 5G uses higher frequencies but still non-ionizing.  
- **Myth**: "Phone cases block all radiation."  
  - *Fact*: Most cases don’t shield RF waves effectively.

---

# Slide 7

## 🎯 Summary & Key Takeaways  
1. Phone radiations are *non-ionizing* but still under study.  
2. Exposure increases with poor signal or prolonged use.  
3. Simple habits can minimize risks.  
4. Stay informed – science evolves!
