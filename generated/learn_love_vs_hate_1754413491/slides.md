## Introduction to love vs hate
- **Love vs hate** are two powerful, opposing emotions—**love** fosters connection and compassion, while **hate** drives division and harm.
- Understanding these emotions is **essential** for building healthy relationships, resolving conflicts, and promoting empathy in society.
- **Did you know?** A Harvard study found that **acts of love and kindness** can reduce stress and improve lifespan, while chronic anger (linked to hate) increases heart disease risk by 19%.
