## Introduction to sphinx egypt
- The **Great Sphinx of Giza** is a **massive limestone statue** with the body of a lion and the head of a human, believed to represent the Pharaoh Khafre.
- It is one of the **oldest and largest monuments** in the world, symbolizing the **power and mystery** of ancient Egyptian civilization.
- The Sphinx is **240 feet long and 66 feet tall**, and its missing nose remains a **historical enigma**—no one knows exactly how or when it was lost.
