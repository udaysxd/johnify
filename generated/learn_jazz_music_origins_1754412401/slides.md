## Introduction to jazz music origins
- **Jazz music origins** trace back to the late 19th and early 20th centuries in **New Orleans**, blending African rhythms, European harmonies, and American blues.
- Jazz is culturally significant as it represents **innovation, freedom, and diversity**, shaping modern music genres like rock, hip-hop, and R&B.
- An interesting fact: The **first jazz recording**, "Livery Stable Blues" by the Original Dixieland Jass Band, was released in **1917**, sparking the genre's global spread.

## Slide 2
- **Blues and Ragtime Roots**: Jazz emerged from the blending of **African-American blues** and **ragtime** in the late 19th century. This fusion created the rhythmic and improvisational foundation that defines jazz.
- **New Orleans Birthplace**: Jazz originated in **New Orleans**, where diverse cultural influences (African, Caribbean, European) mixed in the city's vibrant music scene. This cultural melting pot made the city the perfect incubator for jazz.
- **Improvisation as Core**: Early jazz prioritized **spontaneous improvisation**, allowing musicians to express individuality within a collective structure. This innovation set jazz apart from other musical genres of the time.
