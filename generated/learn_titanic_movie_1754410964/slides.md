## Introduction to titanic movie
- **Titanic (1997)** is a **romantic disaster film** directed by James Cameron, blending a fictional love story with the real-life sinking of the RMS Titanic in 1912.
- The film is **culturally significant** for its record-breaking success, winning **11 Academy Awards**, and its enduring impact on pop culture.
- An **interesting fact**: Titanic held the title of **highest-grossing film of all time** for over a decade, earning **$2.2 billion** worldwide.

## Slide 2
- **Historical and fictional blend** - The movie mixes **real events** (like the Titanic's sinking) with a **fictional love story** (Jack and Rose). This makes the tragedy feel personal and emotionally engaging for viewers.
- **Class divide theme** - The film highlights the **contrast between rich and poor** passengers, showing how social status affected survival chances. This adds depth to the story and reflects real-life inequalities.
- **Human cost of hubris** - The disaster resulted from **overconfidence** in the ship's "unsinkable" design. The film shows how arrogance led to preventable deaths, serving as a cautionary tale.
