## Which feature is unique to the Rolex Perpetual movement: Core Concept

![Which feature is unique to the Rolex Perpetual movement Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/d4920bf426d4c20606f844cd7d97dbbc/800/300)

The Rolex Perpetual movement is unique for its automatic winding capability, harnessing the motion of the wearer's wrist to keep the watch running without the need for manual winding or batteries\.
