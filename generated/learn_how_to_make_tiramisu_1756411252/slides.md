## Introduction to Tiramisu
- Classic Italian dessert meaning "pick me up"
- Layers of coffee-soaked ladyfingers and mascarpone cream
- **Key components**: Savoiardi biscuits, mascarpone, eggs, sugar, espresso, cocoa

<!-- NARRATION: Welcome to the world of tiramisu, a beloved Italian dessert whose name means "pick me up"—and it certainly will! This delightful treat consists of layers of espresso-soaked ladyfinger biscuits and rich, creamy mascarpone. The key ingredients you'll need include Savoiardi biscuits, fresh mascarpone cheese, eggs, sugar, strong espresso, and cocoa powder. Each component plays a vital role in creating its signature flavor and texture. Let's begin our delicious journey into making this classic dessert! -->

## Preparing the Mascarpone Cream
- Separate eggs carefully (no yolk in whites)
- Whip egg whites to **stiff peaks**; set aside
- Beat egg yolks with sugar until pale and thick (**ribbon stage**)
- Gently fold mascarpone into yolk mixture until smooth
- Fold whipped egg whites into mascarpone mixture in **three batches**

<!-- NARRATION: Now, let's prepare the mascarpone cream, the heart of our tiramisu. Start by carefully separating the eggs, ensuring no yolk gets into the whites. Whip the egg whites until they form stiff peaks, then set them aside. Next, beat the egg yolks with sugar until they reach the ribbon stage—pale, thick, and creamy. Gently fold the mascarpone into the yolk mixture until smooth. Finally, incorporate the whipped egg whites in three batches to keep the mixture light and airy. This step is crucial for that perfect creamy texture! -->

---

## Assembling the Layers: Part 2
- Repeat dipping and arranging second layer of ladyfingers
- Spread remaining mascarpone cream evenly
- **Final dusting** of cocoa powder covering entire surface
- Cover with plastic wrap (touching surface to prevent skin)

<!-- NARRATION: With our mascarpone cream ready, it's time to assemble the layers. After placing the first layer of coffee-dipped ladyfingers, add half the cream. Now, repeat with a second layer of dipped biscuits and spread the remaining mascarpone cream evenly on top. For the finishing touch, dust the entire surface with a generous layer of cocoa powder. Cover the tiramisu with plastic wrap, making sure it touches the surface to prevent a skin from forming. This ensures it sets beautifully in the refrigerator. -->

---

## Serving Techniques
- Use sharp knife dipped in hot water for clean cuts
- Serve chilled in rectangular portions
- Garnish with extra cocoa or chocolate shavings
- **Presentation tip**: Wipe plate edges clean

<!-- NARRATION: Serving tiramisu properly enhances the experience. For clean, neat slices, use a sharp knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry between cuts. Serve the dessert well-chilled, in rectangular portions. You can garnish each serving with an extra dusting of cocoa or some chocolate shavings for an elegant touch. Remember to wipe the edges of the plate clean for a professional presentation. These small details make your tiramisu not only taste amazing but look impressive too! -->

## Variations and Adaptations
- **Alcohol-free**: Replace with espresso only
- **Flavor twists**: Add orange zest or cinnamon to cream
- **Dietary**: Gluten-free ladyfingers or reduced sugar
- **Presentation**: Individual servings in glasses

<!-- NARRATION: Tiramisu is versatile and can be adapted to suit different tastes and dietary needs. For an alcohol-free version, simply use espresso without any liqueur. To add a twist, incorporate orange zest or a pinch of cinnamon into the cream. For dietary preferences, try gluten-free ladyfingers or reduce the sugar. You can also present it in individual glasses for a stylish, personalized serving. These variations allow everyone to enjoy this classic dessert in their own way! -->

---

## Final Pro Tips
- Use **fresh, high-quality ingredients** especially mascarpone
- Room temperature eggs whip better
- Brew espresso strong (it dilutes when absorbed)
- Taste cream mixture and adjust sweetness if needed

<!-- NARRATION: Here are some final pro tips to ensure your tiramisu turns out perfectly. Always use fresh, high-quality ingredients, especially the mascarpone, as it greatly affects the flavor. Remember that room temperature eggs whip up much better than cold ones. Brew your espresso strong because it will dilute slightly when absorbed by the biscuits. Lastly, taste the cream mixture before assembling and adjust the sweetness if needed. Following these tips will help you create a truly exceptional tiramisu every time! -->
