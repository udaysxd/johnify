## Top 10 Richest Countries In The World By Gdp Per Capita
*   **Luxembourg leads** with a GDP per capita of approximately $135,683 (2023 IMF est.), driven by its robust banking and financial services sector.
*   **Ireland ($112,248)** and **Switzerland ($102,866)** follow, with Ireland's figure influenced by foreign multinational corporate tax structures.
*   **Norway ($99,266)** and **Singapore ($91,100)** rank highly, with wealth from sovereign oil funds and a global trade & finance hub, respectively.
*   **Qatar ($84,514)** and **United Arab Emirates ($78,255)** derive significant wealth from petroleum and natural gas reserves and related industries.
*   **The United States ($80,035)** and **Denmark ($69,273)** represent large, diverse advanced economies with high productivity and innovation.
*   GDP per capita measures economic output per person and is a strong indicator of a nation's standard of living and economic prosperity.

## Core Ideas
- GDP per capita measures average economic output per person, calculated by dividing a country's total GDP by its population.
- Small, often resource-rich or financial hub nations like Luxembourg ($135,683) and Ireland ($112,248) dominate the top rankings.
- Rankings can be presented using nominal GDP (market exchange rates) or PPP (Purchasing Power Parity), which adjusts for cost of living differences.
- High GDP per capita does not always equate to uniform wealth distribution; it is a national average that can mask internal inequality.
- Common traits among top countries include small populations, strategic industries (finance, technology), and/or significant natural resources (e.g., oil in Qatar, Norway).
