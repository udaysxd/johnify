## Understanding: What is phytoremediation primarily used for

![What is phytoremediation primarily used for](https://picsum.photos/seed/dc92a193c7e8147675c5bfb518a2eb4e/800/300)

**Question:** What is phytoremediation primarily used for?

**Correct Answer:** Removing contaminants from soil, water, or air

---

## What is phytoremediation primarily used for: Core Concept

![What is phytoremediation primarily used for Concept](https://picsum.photos/seed/f538ce4a41c86135a5d99b2d4feda138/800/300)

Phytoremediation is a process that uses living plants to clean up soil, air, and water contaminated with hazardous chemicals\. It is not primarily used for increasing agricultural yields, enhancing crop flavors, or preventing plant diseases\.

*Key points to remember:*
- Phytoremediation is a process that uses living plants to clean up soil, air, and water contaminated with hazardous chemicals\.
