## Sydney Opera House
- A UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- Distinctive sail-like architecture.
- World-renowned performing arts venue.
- Must-see for any visitor.
- Offers tours and performances.

## Sydney Harbour Bridge
- The 'Coathanger' of Sydney.
- One of the world's largest steel arch bridges.
- Offers breathtaking views from the top.
- Popular for bridge climbs.
- Connects Sydney CBD to the North Shore.

<!-- NARRATION: Next, marvel at the Sydney Harbour Bridge, affectionately known as the 'Coathanger'. This impressive structure offers incredible views and exciting opportunities for adventure like bridge climbing. -->

## Bondi Beach
- Australia's most famous beach.
- Golden sands and rolling waves.
- Vibrant surf culture.
- Coastal walks offer scenic beauty.
- Great for swimming, sunbathing, and dining.
