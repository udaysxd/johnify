## The ABC Quick Check: Your Safety First!
- A - Air: Check tire pressure.
- B - Brakes: Test front and rear.
- C - Chain: Ensure it's clean & oiled.
- Quick & easy safety habit.
- Essential for every ride.

<!-- NARRATION: Before you hit the road, a quick safety check can make all the difference. The ABC Quick Check is a simple yet vital habit to ensure your bike is in good working order. Let's go through it together! -->

---

## A for Air: Tire Pressure is Key
- Check tire sidewall for recommended pressure (PSI).
- Underinflated tires are sluggish and puncture-prone.
- Overinflated tires offer a rougher ride and less grip.
- Use a pump with a gauge for accuracy.
- Both tires need to be properly inflated.

<!-- NARRATION: First up is 'A' for Air, referring to your tires. Properly inflated tires are crucial for a smooth and safe ride. Check the recommended pressure on your tire's sidewall and use a pump with a gauge to ensure they're just right. -->

---

## B for Brakes: Stopping Power Matters
- Squeeze brake levers – they shouldn't touch the handlebars.
- Spin wheels to ensure brakes aren't rubbing.
- Check brake pads for wear.
- Listen for squeaks or grinding sounds.
- Ensure both front and rear brakes work effectively.

<!-- NARRATION: Next, we have 'B' for Brakes, your most important safety feature. Squeeze your brake levers to make sure they feel firm and don't go all the way to the handlebars. Spin your wheels to check for any rubbing from the brake pads. -->

---

## C for Chain: Smooth Sailing
- Is your chain clean? Grime slows you down.
- Is it properly lubricated? Dry chains wear out faster.
- Gently push the chain – there should be slight slack.
- A well-maintained chain shifts smoothly.
- This prolongs the life of your drivetrain.

<!-- NARRATION: The 'C' in our check stands for Chain. A clean and lubricated chain will make your pedaling much smoother and more efficient. Give it a quick look and feel to ensure it's running well and has a little bit of gentle slack. -->

---

## Beyond ABC: Other Quick Checks
- Quick-release levers: Are they secure?
- Handlebars: Tight and straight?
- Saddle: Firmly in place?
- Listen for unusual noises.
- A few extra seconds can prevent accidents.

<!-- NARRATION: While the ABCs cover the essentials, don't forget a few other quick checks. Ensure your quick-release levers are secure, your handlebars are straight and tight, and your saddle is firmly in place. These small checks add up to big safety! -->
