## Introduction to trance
- **Trance** is a genre of electronic dance music (EDM) characterized by **repetitive melodic phrases**, **build-ups**, and **emotional crescendos**, often inducing a hypnotic or euphoric state.
- It’s **culturally significant** for uniting global audiences in festivals like ASOT and fostering a sense of **shared transcendence** through music.
- An interesting fact: The **longest trance DJ set** lasted 60 hours, set by DJ Dariush in 2023, showcasing the genre’s enduring energy.
