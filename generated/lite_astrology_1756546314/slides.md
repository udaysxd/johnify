## Astrology
- **Core Principle:** A system linking celestial positions at birth to personality and life events, based on the 12 zodiac signs (e.g., Aries, Taurus, Gemini) and planetary movements.
- **The Natal Chart:** A personalized map of the sky at your exact moment of birth, plotting planets within 12 astrological houses, each governing a life area (e.g., 7th house for relationships).
- **The Four Elements:** Signs are categorized into four groups: Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), and Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces), influencing core traits.
- **Planetary Influences:** Each planet rules specific aspects; e.g., Mercury governs communication, Venus rules love, and Mars influences energy and drive.
- **Daily Guidance:** Astrologers create horoscopes by tracking current planetary transits, such as Mercury retrograde (occurring ~3-4 times a year), which is associated with communication mishaps.

## Core Ideas
- The zodiac divides the celestial sphere into 12 equal 30° segments, each named after a constellation the sun appears to pass through.
- A natal chart is a snapshot of planetary positions at your exact birth time, date, and location, forming a unique astrological blueprint.
- The "Big Three" are your Sun sign (core identity), Moon sign (emotional inner world), and Ascendant/Rising sign (outward personality mask).
- Planets represent different energies (e.g., Mars for action, Venus for love) and their placement by sign and house indicates how these energies are expressed.
- Aspects are the geometric angles (e.g., conjunctions, squares, trines) between planets, showing how their energies interact, either harmoniously or challenging.

## How To Learn
- **Start with your Sun sign** (determined by birth date) to understand core personality traits, then explore your Moon (emotional nature) and Rising sign (external persona).

## Examples
- **Aspect Interpretation**: A 120° trine between Sun and Moon indicates natural harmony between will and emotions, often seen in charts of well-adjusted individuals.
- **House Significance**: Planets in the 10th House (career/public life) heavily influence one's professional path and public reputation.
- **Rising Sign Impact**: A person with a Leo Ascendant will often project confidence, creativity, and a need for recognition upon first meeting.
- **Transit Timing**: Saturn's 2.5-year transit through a house brings lessons and restructuring to that life area, demanding maturity and discipline.

## Summary
- Zodiac signs divide into 12 sun signs based on birth date (e.g., Aries: Mar 21–Apr 19)
- Birth charts require exact time, date, and location to map planetary positions in 12 houses
- The "Big Three" are your Sun (core self), Moon (emotions), and Rising (outer persona) signs
- Planetary aspects (e.g., conjunctions, squares) describe angular relationships influencing traits
- Transits (current planet movements) are analyzed against your natal chart for timing events
