## Banana Ripeness: A Spectrum of Flavor
- Bananas change color as they ripen.
- Color indicates sugar content and texture.
- Each stage offers a unique taste experience.
- From starchy to intensely sweet.

<!-- NARRATION: Bananas offer a fascinating journey of flavor as they ripen, changing color and texture along the way. Understanding these stages helps us pick the perfect banana for any occasion, from cooking to enjoying a sweet snack. -->

---

## Green Bananas: The Unripe Stage
- Firm texture, slightly waxy.
- Low sugar, high starch content.
- Mild, somewhat grassy or neutral flavor.
- Good for cooking, like plantains.

<!-- NARRATION: Green bananas are the least ripe, featuring a firm texture and a starchy, neutral flavor. They're less sweet but hold their shape well, making them a good choice for savory dishes and cooking applications. -->

---

## Yellow Bananas: The Perfect Balance
- Smooth texture, easy to peel.
- Sugar levels increase significantly.
- Classic sweet banana flavor.
- Ideal for snacking and baking.

<!-- NARRATION: As bananas turn yellow, they become sweeter and softer, developing that classic, beloved banana taste. This is the prime stage for eating them fresh or incorporating them into various baked goods. -->

---

## Yellow with Brown Spots: Sweetening Up
- Softer texture, easier to mash.
- Starch converts to sugar, very sweet.
- Intensified, rich banana flavor.
- Excellent for smoothies and desserts.

<!-- NARRATION: When your banana starts developing brown spots, it means more starch has converted to sugar, making it incredibly sweet and soft. The flavor becomes more intense, perfect for when you crave a rich, sugary taste. -->

---

## Brown/Black Bananas: Overripe & Flavorful
- Very soft, mushy texture.
- Maximum sweetness, intense caramel notes.
- Flavor can be almost fermented.
- Best for banana bread and purees.

<!-- NARRATION: Fully ripe bananas, with brown or black skins, are at their sweetest with complex, sometimes caramel-like flavors. While their texture is very soft, they are fantastic for moist baked goods and smoothies. -->
