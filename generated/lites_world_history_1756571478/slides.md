## The Dawn of Civilization
- Began around 10,000 BCE with the Agricultural Revolution.
- Shift from nomadic hunter-gatherers to settled farming communities.
- Development of villages, cities, and specialized labor.
- Early civilizations emerged in Mesopotamia, Egypt, Indus Valley, and China.
- These societies developed writing, government, and religion.

## Ancient Empires & Ideas
- Rise of powerful empires like Rome, Greece, Persia, and Han China.
- Spread of philosophies, religions, and scientific advancements.
- Key developments include democracy (Greece), Roman law, and Buddhism.
- Trade routes like the Silk Road connected diverse cultures.
- These empires laid foundations for many modern concepts.

## The Medieval Period
- Also known as the Middle Ages, following the fall of the Western Roman Empire.
- Dominated by feudalism, manorialism, and the influence of the Church.
- Significant events include the Crusades, the Black Death, and the Hundred Years' War.
- Cultural and intellectual centers shifted, with advancements in Islamic civilization.
- Seeds of nation-states and new economic systems were sown.

## The Age of Exploration & Revolution
- European voyages expanded global knowledge and trade networks.
- Columbian Exchange brought new crops, diseases, and people across continents.
- Renaissance and Reformation spurred intellectual and religious change.
- Age of Enlightenment led to revolutions in America and France.
- These events reshaped political structures and global power.

## Modern World & Its Challenges
- Industrial Revolution transformed economies and societies.
- Rise of nationalism and imperialism led to global conflicts.
- World Wars I and II had devastating global impacts.
- The Cold War era introduced new geopolitical tensions.
- Contemporary world faces globalization, technological advancement, and environmental issues.
