## 2. "Bohemian Rhapsody" by Queen
- **Year**: 1975
- **Album**: *A Night at the Opera*
- **Key features**: Operatic sections, multi-part composition, no chorus.
- **Legacy**: Broke conventional songwriting rules; remains a global anthem.

<!-- NARRATION: Next up is 'Bohemian Rhapsody' by Queen, released in 1975 on the album *A Night at the Opera*. Its creativity and boldness continue to inspire musicians and fans alike, making it a true masterpiece in rock history. -->

## 4. "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana
- **Year**: 1991
- **Album**: *Nevermind*
- **Key features**: Grunge sound, quiet-loud dynamics, anti-establishment themes.
- **Legacy**: Catalyzed the grunge movement and 1990s alternative rock.

<!-- NARRATION: Now, let's talk about 'Smells Like Teen Spirit' by Nirvana from 1991, featured on the album *Nevermind*. This track defined the grunge movement with its raw sound, dynamic shifts between quiet and loud, and anti-establishment lyrics. - **Key features**: Mysterious lyrics, dual guitar solos, Latin rhythm.
- **Legacy**: Symbolizes 1970s rock; enduring mystery and musical craftsmanship.

<!-- NARRATION: Moving on to 'Hotel California' by Eagles, released in 1976 on the album of the same name. - **Legacy**: Redefined electric guitar possibilities in rock.

<!-- NARRATION: Here's 'Purple Haze' by The Jimi Hendrix Experience from 1967, off the album *Are You Experienced*.
