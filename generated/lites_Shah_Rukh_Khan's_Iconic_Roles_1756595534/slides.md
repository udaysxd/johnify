## The King of Romance: A Legacy of Love
- Defined the Bollywood romantic hero for a generation.
- 'Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge' (DDLJ) - Raj's enduring charm.
- 'Kuch Kuch Hota Hai' - The lovable Rahul Khanna.
- 'Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham...' - The passionate Rahul Raichand.
- Master of emotional depth and on-screen chemistry.

## Beyond Romance: Versatility in Action
- Challenged conventions with anti-hero roles.
- 'Darr' - The obsessive stalker, Rahul Mehra.
- 'Baazigar' - The vengeful anti-hero, Vicky Malhotra.
- 'Don' - The stylish, ruthless criminal mastermind.
- Demonstrated a remarkable range as an actor.
