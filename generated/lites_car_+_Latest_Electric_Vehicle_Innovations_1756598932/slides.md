## Beyond Lithium-Ion: Battery Breakthroughs
- Solid-state batteries offer higher energy density and safety.
- Improved charging speeds, potentially minutes, not hours.
- Longer lifespan and reduced degradation.
- Exploring alternative chemistries like sodium-ion.

- Wireless charging pads for convenient home and public use.
- Bidirectional charging: EVs powering your home or grid.
