# Slide 2

# Slide 2: Introduction/Overview  
**What is Cocaine?**  
- A powerful **stimulant** drug derived from the coca plant.  
- Used medically as an anesthetic in rare cases, but mostly **illegal** for recreational use.  
- Highly addictive due to its impact on the brain’s reward system.  

**Why Talk About It?**  
- Rising misuse globally.  
- Serious health, legal, and social consequences.

---

# Slide 3

# Slide 3: How Cocaine Works  
**The Science Behind the High**  
- Cocaine **blocks dopamine reuptake** in the brain → intense euphoria.  
- Short-term effects:  
  - Increased energy, alertness.  
  - Elevated heart rate, paranoia.  
- Example: A user may feel "invincible" but crash soon after.  

**Brain Comparison**  
- Normal brain: Balanced dopamine.  
- Cocaine brain: Dopamine overload → addiction risk.

---

# Slide 4

# Slide 4: Short-Term vs. Long-Term Effects  
**Short-Term (Hours After Use)**  
- Euphoria, talkativeness.  
- Risky behavior (e.g., reckless driving).  
- Potential for **heart attack** even in healthy adults.  

**Long-Term (Chronic Use)**  
- Addiction (loss of control over use).  
- **Nasal damage** (if snorted).  
- Mental health issues (anxiety, psychosis).  

**Example:** Famous cases like athletes or celebrities facing downfall due to addiction.

---

# Slide 5

# Slide 5: Legal and Social Consequences  
**Legal Status**  
- **Illegal** in most countries; severe penalties for possession/trafficking.  
- Medical use is **extremely restricted** (e.g., some eye surgeries).  

**Social Impact**  
- Financial ruin (costs up to **$100+/gram**).  
- Strained relationships, job loss.  
- Example: Prison sentences for trafficking.

---

# Slide 6

# Slide 6: Myths vs. Facts  
**Myth:** "Cocaine isn’t addictive if used occasionally."  
**Fact:** Even casual use can lead to dependency.  

**Myth:** "It’s a ‘safe’ party drug."  
**Fact:** It can cause **sudden death** due to heart failure.  

**Myth:** "You can quit anytime."  
**Fact:** Withdrawal is intense (depression, fatigue).

---

# Slide 7

# Slide 7: Summary and Key Takeaways  
1. Cocaine is a **highly addictive** stimulant with severe risks.  
2. Harms both physical/mental health and social stability.  
3. **No safe level** of recreational use.  
4. Seeking help? Resources: SAMHSA, local rehab centers.  

**Final Thought:** *"The temporary high isn’t worth the lifelong consequences."*
