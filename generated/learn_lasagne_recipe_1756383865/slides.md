- **Audience**: Intermediate home cooks familiar with basic techniques

## Preparing the Ragù (Meat Sauce)
- Sauté **soffritto** in olive oil until soft and golden
- Brown **ground meat** thoroughly; deglaze with **red wine** and reduce
- Add **tomato passata**, bay leaf, salt, pepper; simmer **2–3 hours** on low heat
- **Key tip**: Slow cooking develops depth of flavor; stir occasionally

<!-- NARRATION: Let's start with the heart of lasagne: the ragù or meat sauce. Begin by sautéing soffritto in olive oil until golden. Brown your ground meat thoroughly, then deglaze with red wine. Add tomato passata, bay leaf, and seasonings, then simmer for 2–3 hours on low heat. Remember, slow cooking develops incredible depth of flavor, so be patient and stir occasionally for the best results. -->

---

## Preparing Pasta & Cheese
- If using dried pasta, **parboil** sheets al dente; drain and lay flat on towels
- For no-boil sheets, ensure enough sauce moisture to cook pasta during baking
- Grate **Parmigiano-Reggiano** finely; shred or slice mozzarella
- **Pro tip**: Lightly oil boiled sheets to prevent sticking

<!-- NARRATION: Now, prepare your pasta and cheese. If using dried pasta, parboil sheets until al dente, then drain and lay them flat on towels. For no-boil sheets, ensure there's enough sauce moisture. Grate Parmigiano-Reggiano finely and shred your mozzarella. A pro tip: lightly oil boiled pasta sheets to prevent sticking, making assembly smoother and more efficient. -->

---

## Baking & Resting
- Cover with foil; bake at 375°F (190°C) for **25 minutes**
- Remove foil; bake additional 20–25 minutes until golden and bubbly
- **Critical step**: Rest lasagne **15–20 minutes** before slicing to set layers
- **Result**: Clean cuts, distinct layers, optimal texture

<!-- NARRATION: Time for baking and resting! Cover your lasagne with foil and bake at 375°F for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake another 20–25 minutes until golden and bubbly. The critical step: let it rest for 15–20 minutes before slicing. This allows the layers to set, ensuring clean cuts and perfect, distinct layers with optimal texture in every bite. -->

## Variations & Customizations
- **Vegetarian**: Substitute ragù with roasted vegetables and lentil sauce
- **Seafood**: Use white sauce and layers of shrimp, scallops, or salmon
- **Gluten-free**: Employ GF pasta sheets and cornstarch-thickened béchamel
- **Make-ahead**: Assemble unbaked; refrigerate up to 2 days or freeze

<!-- NARRATION: Lasagne is versatile! Try a vegetarian version with roasted vegetables and lentil sauce, or a seafood option with white sauce and shrimp. For gluten-free, use GF pasta and cornstarch-thickened béchamel. You can also assemble ahead and refrigerate for up to 2 days or freeze it. Customize to suit dietary needs or preferences while keeping the classic structure. -->

## Final Pro Tips
- **Quality ingredients matter**: Especially cheese and tomatoes
- **Balance sauces**: Ragù and béchamel should complement, not overwhelm
- **Practice layering**: Even distribution ensures consistent bites
- **Enjoy the process**: Lasagne is a labor of love; results are worth it!
