## What is the primary purpose of pollination in plants: Core Concept

![What is the primary purpose of pollination in plants Concept](C:\johnify\image_cache\79c18dc692f15f5dde31372ba35f38fe.jpg)

The primary purpose of pollination is to facilitate the transfer of pollen from the male parts to the female parts of flowers, enabling the production of seeds and fruits, which are essential for plant reproduction\.

## Which of the following is NOT a common pollinator: Core Concept

![Which of the following is NOT a common pollinator Concept](C:\johnify\image_cache\2b2992fb83c0e85656f73def7c2a4e65.jpg)

Earthworms are not pollinators\. They live in soil and help with decomposition and soil aeration, but they do not transfer pollen between flowers like bees, butterflies, and bats do\.

## Understanding: What type of pollination occurs when pollen is transferred from the anther of one flower to the stigma of another flower on the same plant

![What type of pollination occurs when pollen is transferred from the anther of one flower to the stigma of another flower on the same plant](C:\johnify\image_cache\f7db5411116540a09201703dd8b82453.jpg)

**Question:** What type of pollination occurs when pollen is transferred from the anther of one flower to the stigma of another flower on the same plant?

**Correct Answer:** Self\-pollination

---

## What type of pollination occurs when pollen is transferred from the anther of one flower to the stigma of another flower on the same plant: Core Concept

![What type of pollination occurs when pollen is transferred from the anther of one flower to the stigma of another flower on the same plant Concept](C:\johnify\image_cache\114e02d2c7e87ac215fdb403a661bc7a.jpg)

Self\-pollination occurs when pollen is transferred from the anther to the stigma of the same flower or another flower on the same plant, unlike cross\-pollination which involves different plants\.

*Key points to remember:*
- Key point 1 about the concept
- Key point 2 about the concept
- Key point 3 about the concept
