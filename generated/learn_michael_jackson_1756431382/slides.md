## Michael Jackson
- Born August 29, 1958, in Gary, Indiana; began performing professionally at age 5 with his brothers in The Jackson 5
- Sold over 400 million records worldwide, making him one of the best-selling music artists in history
- Won 13 Grammy Awards and received the Grammy Legend Award in 1993
- Pioneered groundbreaking music videos, including the 14-minute "Thriller" (1983) and "Smooth Criminal" (1987)
- His 1982 album "Thriller" is the best-selling album of all time, with estimated sales of 66 million copies
- Performed the iconic "moonwalk" dance move during a 1983 Motown 25 television special

## Core Ideas
- Pioneered the music video as an art form with "Thriller" (1983), a 14-minute short film that revolutionized the industry and helped the album sell over 70 million copies worldwide.
- Perfected a signature dance style combining precise isolations, sharp angular movements, and iconic steps like the Moonwalk, which he famously debuted in 1983.
- Mastered the recording studio process, often recording dozens of vocal takes for a single song and layering his voice to create a rich, choral effect, as heard on "Man in the Mirror."
- Championed global humanitarian efforts, personally donating over $300 million to charity and co-writing "We Are the World," which raised over $60 million for famine relief.
- Transformed live performance into large-scale spectacle through massive world tours like the "Bad" tour (1987-1989), which played 123 concerts to 4.4 million fans.

## How To Learn
- Analyze 5 distinct vocal techniques from *Thriller* (e.g., staccato beats, breath control, falsetto) and practice each for 15 minutes daily.
- Master the moonwalk by deconstructing it into 4 steps: the backslide, toe-stand, weight transfer, and glide, practicing with a mirror for immediate feedback.
- Study the composition of "Billie Jean" by mapping its structure: 8-bar intro, 16-bar verse, 8-bar pre-chorus, 8-bar chorus, and 4-bar bridge.
- Replicate a specific dance move from the *Smooth Criminal* music video by watching it frame-by-frame at 0.25x speed for precise limb and timing accuracy.
- Record your practice sessions and compare them against the original performance, focusing on one measurable metric like timing (e.g., syncopation accuracy) or vocal pitch.

## Summary
