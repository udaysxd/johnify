## The Majestic Mango: A Tropical Treasure
- Globally loved for its sweet, juicy flesh and vibrant color.
- Native to South Asia, cultivated for over 4,000 years.
- Rich in vitamins A and C, and antioxidants.
- A symbol of hospitality and prosperity in many cultures.

## Alphonso: The King of Mangoes
- Renowned for its rich, creamy texture and sweet, aromatic flavor.
- Saffron-yellow skin, blush of red when ripe.
- Primarily grown in Maharashtra, India.
- Highly prized for its taste and often used in desserts and juices.

<!-- NARRATION: First up is the Alphonso, often called the 'King of Mangoes' for its incredible flavor and creamy texture. Its beautiful saffron-yellow skin often sports a lovely red blush, making it a feast for the eyes as well as the palate. -->

## Kent: A Smooth operator
- Medium to large size with smooth, tender flesh.
- Sweet, mild flavor with low fiber content.
- Green skin turning to a reddish-yellow/orange blush.
- Popular in the United States and Mexico for its consistent quality.

<!-- NARRATION: Next, we have the Kent mango, a smooth operator in the mango world! Its tender, low-fiber flesh offers a pleasingly sweet and mild taste, and its green skin with a reddish-yellow blush makes it visually appealing. -->

## Haden: A Classic American Favorite
- Oval shape with a beautiful yellow skin, blushed with red.
- Sweet and slightly tart flavor with minimal fiber.
- Good for eating fresh, juicing, and salads.
- One of the most popular varieties in Florida.

<!-- NARRATION: The Haden mango is a classic American favorite, known for its attractive yellow skin with a red blush. It offers a delightful balance of sweetness and tartness with minimal fiber, making it versatile for many uses. -->

---

## Ataulfo (Honey Mango): Petite Powerhouse
- Small, kidney-shaped with smooth, buttery flesh.
- Intensely sweet and aromatic, with very little fiber.
- Bright yellow, thin skin that wrinkles slightly when ripe.
- Excellent for smoothies, desserts, and fresh consumption.

<!-- NARRATION: Finally, let's talk about the Ataulfo, also known as the Honey Mango. This petite variety packs a punch with its intensely sweet, buttery flesh and very low fiber, making it perfect for enjoying straight from the fruit or blended into smoothies. -->
