## Electric Vehicles
- **Battery range**: Most new EVs travel 250–350 miles per charge, with some models like the Lucid Air exceeding 500 miles.
- **Charging time**: A Level 2 charger (240V) adds ~25 miles of range per hour; a DC fast charger can provide 80% charge in 20–30 minutes.
- **Cost of ownership**: Electricity costs are roughly 3–4 times cheaper per mile than gasoline, and EVs require less maintenance (no oil changes).
- **Environmental impact**: Over their lifetime, EVs produce 50-70% fewer emissions than gasoline cars, even when accounting for manufacturing and electricity generation.
- **Government incentives**: The U.S. federal tax credit offers up to $7,500 for new EV purchases, with many states providing additional rebates.

## Core Ideas
- **Zero tailpipe emissions**: EVs produce no CO₂ or pollutants while driving, reducing urban air pollution by up to 99% compared to internal combustion engines.
- **High energy efficiency**: Electric motors convert over 77% of electrical energy to power at the wheels, versus 12-30% for gasoline engines.
- **Lower operating costs**: Charging an EV costs roughly 50-70% less per mile than fueling a gasoline car, with fewer moving parts reducing maintenance needs.
- **Regenerative braking**: Recaptures kinetic energy during deceleration, typically adding 10-30% back to the battery and extending range.

## How To Learn
- **Watch 3+ EV review videos** (e.g., Bjørn Nyland, Fully Charged) to understand real-world range, charging speed, and common pros/cons.
- **Use online configurators** (Tesla, Ford, VW) to compare prices, battery sizes (e.g., 60 kWh vs. 82 kWh), and estimate charging costs.
- **Test drive at least two models**; focus on regenerative braking feel, acceleration (0-60 mph times), and interior tech usability.
- **Analyze total cost of ownership**; calculate savings from lower maintenance and electricity costs versus higher upfront price.

## Examples
- **Tesla Model 3**: A leading all-electric sedan with a range of up to 358 miles (WLTP) and a 0-60 mph acceleration in as little as 3.1 seconds.
- **Nissan Leaf**: A widely adopted, affordable EV with a 40 kWh battery offering ~168 miles of range; over 650,000 units sold globally since 2010.
- **Ford F-150 Lightning**: An electric pickup truck with a dual-motor setup producing up to 580 horsepower and a towing capacity of up to 10,000 lbs.
- **Public Charging**: Use apps like PlugShare to locate one of over 60,000 public charging stations in the US; a DC fast charger can add ~200 miles in 30 minutes.
