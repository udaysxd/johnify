# Key Concepts for you

Here are the concepts you might want to review:

---

## Which river runs through Paris?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** The Seine


**Explanation:** The Seine is the river that runs through Paris\. The Thames runs through London, the Rhine through Germany, and the Danube through Eastern Europe\.

---

## What is the name of the famous museum in Paris?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** The Louvre


**Explanation:** The Louvre is the famous museum located in Paris\. The British Museum is in London, the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York, and the Uffizi Gallery in Florence\.

---

## Which of these landmarks is not located in Paris?


[X] **Your answer:** 


[✓] **Correct answer:** Big Ben


**Explanation:** Big Ben is located in London, not Paris\. The Eiffel Tower, Notre\-Dame Cathedral, and Arc de Triomphe are all famous landmarks in Paris\.
