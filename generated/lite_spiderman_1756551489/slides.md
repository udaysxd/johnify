## Spiderman
*   **Real Identity:** Peter Parker, a high school student from Queens, New York, who gained his powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider.
*   **Core Powers:** Superhuman strength (can lift ~10 tons), a "spider-sense" that warns of danger, and the ability to cling to most surfaces.
*   **Web-Shooters:** Wrist-mounted devices of his own invention that fire strong, adhesive webbing (not an organic power in most storylines).
*   **Guiding Principle:** Lives by the lesson "With great power comes great responsibility," often prioritizing his hero duties over his personal life.
*   **Key Villains:** Fights a notorious rogues' gallery including the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, and Venom.

## Core Ideas
- **Radioactive spider bite** grants Peter Parker proportional strength (lifting 10 tons), wall-crawling, and a precognitive "spider-sense."
- **With great power comes great responsibility** is the central moral code, driving him to protect New York City as a hero.
- **Web-shooters** are wrist-mounted devices he invented to swing between skyscrapers and immobilize foes with synthetic webbing.
- **Secret identity** as Peter Parker, a freelance photographer for the Daily Bugle, must be protected to keep loved ones safe.
- **Rogues' gallery** includes villains like Doctor Octopus and Green Goblin, who often possess personal connections to his life.

## How To Learn
- **Watch 3–5 full-length Spider-Man films** to understand character evolution, from Tobey Maguire to Tom Holland.
- **Read 10 key comic issues**, such as Amazing Fantasy #15 and The Night Gwen Stacy Died, for foundational stories.
- **Practice drawing Spider-Man in 3 distinct poses daily** to master anatomy, webbing, and dynamic movement.
- **Join online forums (e.g., Reddit’s r/Spiderman) and analyze 2 fan theories weekly** to deepen critical perspective.
- **Create your own short comic page each month**, applying panel layout, dialogue, and action sequencing techniques.

## Examples
- **Web-slinging mechanics**: Uses wrist-mounted shooters to fire synthetic webbing (tensile strength ~120 psi) for urban traversal.
- **Spider-Sense ability**: Pre-cognitive tingling alerts him to danger with a 2–3 second warning, allowing for split-second dodges.
- **Wall-crawling adhesion**: Can support his body weight (167 lbs) on virtually any surface through controlled electrostatic force.
- **Superhuman strength**: Can lift approximately 10 tons, enabling him to stop a moving city bus with webbing and brute force.

## Summary
- First appeared in *Amazing Fantasy* #15 (August 1962), created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko
- Bitten by a radioactive spider, granting proportional spider strength (10-ton lift), wall-crawling, and precognitive "spider-sense"
- Motto: "With great power there must also come great responsibility," from his late Uncle Ben
- Primary villains include Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, and Venom
- Has been portrayed by 3 main live-action actors: Tobey Maguire, Andrew Garfield, and Tom Holland
