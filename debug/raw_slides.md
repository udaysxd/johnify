# THE SPEED OF LIGHT IN VACUUM  
- The speed of light in a vacuum is **299,792,458 metres per second (m/s)**.  
- It is a universal constant, often rounded to **3.00 × 10⁸ m/s** for calculations.  
- Light travels slower in other mediums (e.g., glass or water).  

**Worked Example**:  
How far does light travel in 5 seconds in a vacuum?  
*Solution*:  
Distance = Speed × Time = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s × 5 s = **1.5 × 10⁹ m**.  

**Practice Question**:  
Light takes 8 minutes to reach Earth from the Sun. Calculate the distance between them (speed of light = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s).

# VITAMIN PRODUCED BY SUNLIGHT  
- **Vitamin D** is produced when skin is exposed to sunlight (UVB rays).  
- It helps absorb calcium for strong bones and teeth.  
- Deficiency can lead to **rickets** (in children) or **osteomalacia** (in adults).  

**Worked Example**:  
Why might someone living in a cloudy climate need Vitamin D supplements?  
*Solution*:  
Less sunlight exposure → reduced Vitamin D synthesis → supplements prevent deficiency.  

**Practice Question**:  
Name **two** dietary sources of Vitamin D besides sunlight.
